Turn on any game and count how long it takes before someone mentions a line, a prop or a promo code. It doesn't take long.

Betting has worked its way into almost everything about watching sports. Some fans love it. A lot of us just miss when the only number that mattered was the score.

## What you'll find here

- News on the stories that actually matter to fans
- Takes from writers who care who wins, not who covers
- **Worth Watching**, our weekly ranking of the best games to put on, with no spreads attached

## What you won't

No odds. No spreads. No parlays. No sportsbook ads, no promo codes and no betting partners. We run regular ads to keep the lights on, but we will never take money from a gambling company.

> Just the game. Never the line.

Follow along on X and sign up for the weekly. We're glad you're here.
