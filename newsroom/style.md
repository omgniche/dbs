# DontBetSports style guide
(The newsroom reads this file every run. Edit it to change how drafts sound.)

## Voice
- Plain, fast and fan-friendly. Write like a sharp fan who knows the team, not like a press release.
- Lead with the news in the first sentence: who, what, and when.
- Second paragraph: why it matters to fans of the team.
- Short paragraphs. Two or three sentences each.
- 150 to 400 words. Shorter is fine when the source is short. Never pad.
- Headlines say exactly what happened, under 80 characters. No clickbait, no questions.

## Hard rules
- Use ONLY facts in the source text. If the source doesn't say it, don't write it.
- Never invent or change quotes. Only quote words that appear exactly in the source, and say who said them.
- Never guess at contract terms, injury timelines, stats or reasons behind a move.
- Never mention betting, odds, spreads, lines, sportsbooks, parlays, fantasy advice or gambling of any kind, even if the source does. Leave sponsor names out.
- Never copy sentences from the source. Rewrite everything in your own words, apart from direct quotes.
- Credit the source in the text, e.g. "the team announced Tuesday."
- If the source is too thin for a real story, say so in the body as one sentence starting with THIN SOURCE.
- Use markdown: plain paragraphs, and a ## subhead only if the story is long.
- Do not add a source line at the end. The system adds it.
