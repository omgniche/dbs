The San Francisco 49ers are acquiring wide receiver Keenan Allen from the Indianapolis Colts in exchange for a 2027 sixth-round pick, NFL Network and ESPN reported Tuesday.

The Niners need the help. Their receiver room is thin, and they are still without Mike Evans (rib/Achilles) and Deebo Samuel (hamstring). Jordan Watkins was Brock Purdy's top target last week, and Demarcus Robinson, Brandin Cooks and Jacob Cowing have all been getting significant snaps.

Allen is 34 and not as steady as he used to be. He still runs good routes and can be trusted on third downs. He has 40 catches for 351 yards and a touchdown in eight games this season.

He signed with Indianapolis in August to help a banged-up receiver group. Now he is headed to another one.

Allen has moved around lately. After a Pro Bowl season in 2023, he was traded to Chicago for a fourth-round pick, then went back to Los Angeles in 2025. Over 14 NFL seasons he has 1,095 catches for 12,402 yards and 71 touchdowns. He made six Pro Bowls with the Chargers.

The move also gives San Francisco several of the league's top active pass catchers, per NFL Research. Allen leads active wide receivers in receptions, and Evans leads them in receiving yards with 13,472. Christian McCaffrey leads active running backs in catches, yards and touchdowns, with 662 catches for 5,660 yards and 37 scores. George Kittle has 57 receiving touchdowns, second among active tight ends behind Travis Kelce's 84.
