The San Francisco 49ers have acquired wide receiver Keenan Allen from the Indianapolis Colts in exchange for a 2027 sixth-round pick, the team announced Nov. 11.

The 49ers are getting a veteran with a long track record. Allen has caught 1,095 passes for 12,402 yards and 71 touchdowns over a 14-season career, and he has made six Pro Bowls (2018-22, 2024). He is 34 years old.

This season, Allen played eight games for the Colts, starting four of them. He finished with 40 catches for 351 yards and one touchdown.

The Chargers drafted Allen in the third round (76th overall) in 2013, and he won PFWA Offensive Rookie of the Year that season. He played for the Chargers from 2013 to 2023 and again in 2025, with a stop in Chicago with the Bears in 2024 before landing in Indianapolis in 2026. In 2017 he was named AP Comeback Player of the Year after a career-best 1,393 receiving yards.

He has also been productive in the playoffs, with 23 catches for 361 yards and three touchdowns in six postseason games.

## Jack Jones signed to practice squad

In a separate move, San Francisco signed cornerback Jack Jones to its practice squad. Jones appeared in seven games this season and recorded 11 tackles and three passes defensed before the 49ers released him on Oct. 28.
