The 49ers will be without Brock Purdy on Sunday against the Seahawks. Coach Kyle Shanahan said Friday that the quarterback is out with an ankle sprain, and Mac Jones will start. Kickoff at Levi's Stadium is 4:25 p.m. ET.

This is a big one for San Francisco. The 49ers are 9-1, the best record in the NFC, and Seattle is right behind them in the NFC West. Losing one of the NFL's best quarterbacks for a rivalry game is a real blow.

Purdy didn't practice this week after getting hurt in the Week 11 win over the Vikings in Mexico City. There is good news, though. Shanahan said Purdy doesn't need surgery and that he's confident the QB can return in Week 13 against the Giants.

It's the first game Purdy has missed this season. Last year he sat out nearly half of San Francisco's games with various injuries.

## What Purdy has done so far

Through 10 games, Purdy leads all QBs in success rate (57.9), yards per attempt (9.0) and passer rating (118.2). He has completed 71% of his throws for 2,624 yards, 23 touchdowns and five interceptions.

## Jones has filled in before

Jones has done this job in San Francisco. He started eight games for Purdy last season and the Niners went 5-3, which kept them afloat long enough to make the postseason once Purdy returned. Across 11 appearances, he completed 69.6% of his passes for 2,151 yards, 13 touchdowns and six interceptions.

He'll face a tough test. Sam Darnold returns for the reigning Super Bowl champion Seahawks after missing two games with quad and rib injuries. It's also Seattle's first game at Levi's Stadium since winning the title.
