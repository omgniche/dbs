The San Francisco 49ers have ruled out quarterback Brock Purdy for Sunday's Week 12 game against the Seattle Seahawks, and Mac Jones will start in his place, the team announced Friday after its final practice of the week.

Purdy hurt his ankle in the second quarter of the 35-10 win over the Vikings in Mexico City and did not practice this week. That is a tough break for a 9-1 team that returns home to Levi's Stadium looking to sweep Seattle. Purdy had just picked up his second NFC Offensive Player of the Week honor of the season.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan called it more of a mid-ankle sprain than a low-ankle sprain and said it hasn't healed as fast as the team wanted. Surgery isn't needed, he said, and he's optimistic about Purdy's availability next week, though it's too early to tell.

Jones has been here before. He led San Francisco to five wins in eight starts in 2025, but this will be his first start against Seattle as a 49er. Kurtis Rourke moves up to the backup job.

"He's had a great week," Shanahan said. "We went through this a lot with him last year, so everyone's got a ton of confidence in Mac."

Jones said he has prepared like a starter all season. "It's an amazing defense. I've watched from the side for multiple games against this defense, but going up against them is going to be a huge challenge, and I'm looking forward to it," he said. "It's an opportunity I'm not going to take lightly."

## Rest of the injury report

WR Jordan Watkins (groin) is also out after missing all three practices.

Five players are questionable:

- WR Mike Evans (Achilles), who scored in his return last week and was limited Thursday and Friday
- LB Dre Greenlaw (neck), questionable for the second straight week
- CB Upton Stout (shoulder, neck)
- DB Siran Neal (knee, foot)
- OL Austen Pleasants (knee)

Pleasants is working his way back from Injured Reserve, and Shanahan said a decision on activating him is expected Saturday. That matters because the team put OL Colton McKivitz on IR Wednesday with a season-ending ankle injury. Pleasants has been practicing exclusively at tackle. "He is versatile enough to play all those spots. He proved that in training camp," Shanahan said.

CB Nate Hobbs was activated from IR on Wednesday and is off the injury report.

San Francisco beat Seattle 32-29 at Lumen Field in Week 5. Sunday is also the 49ers' My Cause My Cleats game. Final gameday statuses will come 90 minutes before kickoff.
