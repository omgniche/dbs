Lead with the news in the first sentence: who, what and when. Readers and Google both decide in the first line whether this answers their question.

Second paragraph: why it matters. Context, what it means for the team, what happens next.

## Use subheads for longer stories

Break the story into sections with two pound signs, like the line above. Google uses these to understand the page.

- Bullet points work with a dash and a space
- **Bold** uses two asterisks on each side
