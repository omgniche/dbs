Detroit Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson broke his silence Thursday on social media, days after a torn ACL ended his season. He got hurt last Sunday while leaping in celebration of a sack of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye.

"I honestly can’t believe it," Hutchinson wrote. "I’ve jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me."

This is the second time in three seasons that a leg injury has cut short the Pro Bowler's year.

Hutchinson played nine games this year. He had six sacks, 24 tackles, six tackles for loss, 19 QB hits, a pass defensed and a forced fumble. His 50 QB pressures were the second-most in the league at the time, one behind Defensive Player of the Year favorite Will Anderson Jr.

Detroit now has to find pass-rush production elsewhere while it pushes for the playoffs. The hope is that Hutchinson returns in 2027 the way he did after his previous knee injury.

He sounded determined in his post. "Two things I know for sure: 1. I always come back stronger. 2. That’s the last time I go airborne," Hutchinson wrote.

He also left a message for his teammates: "Hold down the fort til I’m back, boys."
