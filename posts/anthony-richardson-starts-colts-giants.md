Anthony Richardson Sr. will start at quarterback for the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday against the New York Giants at Lucas Oil Stadium, Colts.com reported Friday. It will be his first start since Week 16 of the 2024 season.

The move comes after the Colts waived Daniel Jones last week. Indianapolis enters Week 12 ranked 26th in the league in scoring at 19.2 points per game.

Richardson's road back has been bumpy. He missed the last two games of 2024 with a back injury, lost the starting job to Jones in 2025 training camp, and landed on injured reserve last October after a freak accident during pregame band work. Colts.com notes he has thrown only six passes over the past two seasons.

"I'm blessed. I'm grateful for the opportunity. I'm ready to showcase what I can do and go out there and have fun," Richardson said.

Head coach Shane Steichen pointed to the quarterback's running ability. "He's got a unique skillset," Steichen said. "Obviously, he's really good running the football.
