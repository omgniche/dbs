Nathan MacKinnon will miss one month and Cale Makar will miss two weeks, Avalanche coach Jared Bednar said Saturday before Colorado's game at the Montreal Canadiens. Both players suffered lower-body injuries in the second period of Thursday's 4-3 overtime loss to the Ottawa Senators, according to NHL.com.

The Avalanche are losing their top scorer and one of their top defensemen at the same time. MacKinnon leads the team and is fifth in the NHL with 30 points (10 goals, 20 assists) in 20 games. Makar is third on Colorado and tied for third among NHL defensemen with 23 points in 19 games.

MacKinnon grabbed his right side after trying a reverse hit on Senators defenseman Artem Zub. He sat on the bench for a few minutes and then went down the tunnel. Makar played a four-second shift, went to the locker room, came back for a 23-second shift, and stayed on the bench the rest of the period. He had left practice Nov. 8 for precautionary reasons.

Bednar said the loss hurts in more than one way. "When I think about those two guys, it's like every matchup, they take it on, now someone else has to be able to step up and do that," he said. "And then you are losing the depth down the lineup as you kind of do that because everyone is elevated."

Forward Martin Necas took the challenge head on. "It's a big task, but we are all excited for it," he said.

Artturi Lehkonen was more direct about the hole in the lineup. "Missing two of the best players in the world, you can't really replace that," he said Saturday.

## Two stars, big résumés

MacKinnon was a Hart Trophy finalist last season after leading the league with 53 goals. He finished with 127 points, third in the NHL. He has topped 100 points in each of the past four seasons.

Makar has won the Norris Trophy twice and has been a finalist in each of the past six seasons. He also won the Conn Smythe Trophy in 2022, when Colorado won the Stanley Cup.

Colorado is 11-6-3 and third in the Central Division.
