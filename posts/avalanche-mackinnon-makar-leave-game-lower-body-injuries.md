Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar both left the Colorado Avalanche's game against the Ottawa Senators on Thursday with lower-body injuries, NHL.com reported. Both exits came in the second period.

MacKinnon leads the team and is fifth in the NHL with 30 points (10 goals, 20 assists) in 20 games. Makar is third on Colorado and third among NHL defensemen with 23 points (five goals, 18 assists) in 19 games.

MacKinnon's last shift ended with 8:15 left in the second period. He grabbed his right side after a hit from Senators defenseman Artem Zub. He stayed on the bench for a few minutes, then went down the tunnel.

Makar left with 5:20 remaining in the period. NHL.com noted that he left practice on Sunday, four days earlier, for precautionary reasons.

The Avalanche are 11-6-2 and sit third in the Central Division. The NHL.com report did not include an update on either player's status.
