Nathan MacKinnon is expected to miss about 12 weeks with a lower-body injury, Colorado Avalanche coach Jared Bednar said Saturday. The star center was first projected to be out one month.

That's a big blow for Colorado. MacKinnon leads the team with 30 points (10 goals, 20 assists) in 20 games. The new timeline rules him out of the NHL Winter Classic against the Utah Mammoth at Rice-Eccles Stadium on Dec. 31, and it could keep him out through NHL All-Star Weekend on Feb. 5-6 in Elmont, New York.

MacKinnon got hurt in the second period of a 4-3 overtime loss at Ottawa on Nov. 12. Bednar said the update came after further medical opinions.

"He saw specialists, saw some other doctors after we returned, and we got a new timeline on him," Bednar said. "It'll be approximately 12 weeks."

Bednar also said the team will work on a plan to try to beat that timeline, though he called it "just a timeline we've been given."

The Avalanche are 13-7-3 and 2-1-0 without MacKinnon. Last season he was a Hart Trophy finalist, leading the league with 53 goals and finishing third with 127 points.

## Makar closer to returning

There was better news on defenseman Cale Makar, who was hurt in the same game in Ottawa. He was expected to be out two weeks, and Bednar said he will skate Sunday. Makar has 23 points (five goals, 18 assists) in 19 games.

Bednar said Makar could play Wednesday against the Montreal Canadiens, but he wouldn't promise it.

"He's trending in the right direction, but I can't say for certain if he'll be in against Montreal or not," Bednar said.

The full team has the day off Monday, then practices Tuesday, according to Bednar, as reported by NHL.com.
