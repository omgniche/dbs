Bears tight end Colston Loveland was ruled out with a knee injury in the first half of Thursday's game against the Lions, according to NFL.com's league news roundup.

The Bears came into the game at 8-2. The roundup gives only the injury and the ruling, with no word on how it happened, how serious it is or how long Loveland might be out.

## Elsewhere around the league

In Pittsburgh, the Steelers activated wideout Michael Pittman from injured reserve, and he carries no injury designation into Friday's game against the Broncos. The team also ruled out linebacker Alex Highsmith (ankle) and guard Gennings Dunker (knee).

In Washington, the Commanders added wideout Brandon Aiyuk to their practice squad, the roundup noted.
