Connor Bedard will miss at least the next four games for the Chicago Blackhawks, and the team is still deciding whether his right shoulder needs surgery, NHL.com reported Thursday.

Coach Jeff Blashill said the club is weighing rehab against surgery and will have more once a decision is made. As for Thursday's home game against Columbus and the three-game road trip that follows, he was blunt: "He definitely won't play tonight and definitely won't play on the road trip."

That is a big hit for a Blackhawks team sitting at 6-13-2. Bedard, the 21-year-old captain, leads Chicago with 18 points (seven goals, 11 assists) in 15 games. After the Columbus game, the Blackhawks visit Calgary on Saturday, Vancouver on Sunday and Seattle on Wednesday.

## How it happened

Bedard got hurt in Tuesday's 6-3 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs. He was lining up for a faceoff against Nick Paul 54 seconds into the third period when Paul's stick hit his right hand as he set his own stick down. Bedard yelled in pain and went straight to the locker room. He had scored his seventh goal of the season in the second period, putting Chicago up 3-2 at the time.

It is his third shoulder injury in less than a year. He hurt his left shoulder in an offseason workout in Vancouver on July 2 and had surgery a few days later. He was first expected back in November but returned Oct. 13.

His first right shoulder injury also came on a faceoff, against St. Louis on Dec. 12, 2025. He returned Jan. 9 but did not take faceoffs in a game until Feb. 26. Blashill said Wednesday that in both cases Bedard hit the opponent's stick on the way down, adding, "It was the exact same thing in a lot of ways."

Blashill also said the team knew there was a high risk of the shoulder coming back out. He said taking faceoffs is part of being a center and that the team will explore ways to take them without lifting the stick and bringing it down.

The No. 1 pick in the 2023 NHL Draft, Bedard has 221 points (82 goals, 139 assists) in 233 games with Chicago.
