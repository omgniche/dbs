The Buffalo Bills are listing seven players as inactive for their Week 11 home game against the Miami Dolphins, which kicks off at 1 p.m.

The inactive list includes WR Skyler Bell, ILB Dorian Williams, CB Christian Benford, OLB Mike Danna, T/G Jude Bowry, TE Dalton Kincaid and DL T.J. Sanders.

The bigger news for fans is who is playing. DL Ed Oliver is active after being a late addition to the injury report Saturday with an oblique injury. S Damar Hamlin and TE Jackson Hawes were both listed as questionable and are also active.

Buffalo also elevated two players from the practice squad: ILB Shaq Thompson and CB Tre'Davious White. Both are active for the division matchup at Highmark Stadium.
