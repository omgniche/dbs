Connor Bedard is back on the Blackhawks' active list. The team announced Tuesday that it activated the forward from injured non-roster, where he had been listed with an upper-body injury.

Additionally, the Blackhawks assigned forward Nick Lardis to the Rockford IceHogs of the American Hockey League.

Bedard's return matters because of how he played last season. The 21-year-old set career highs in goals (30), assists (45) and points (75) over 69 games in 2025-26. He led Chicago in assists and points that year and ranked second on the team in goals, according to the team.

Lardis, also 21, heads to Rockford after a short stretch with the big club. He has two assists in six games with the Blackhawks this season.

The announcement didn't say whether Bedard will play tonight. Chicago hosts the Pittsburgh Penguins at the United Center, with puck drop at 7 p.m. CT.

Fans can watch in-market on CHSN, or out of market on ESPN+. The game is also on WGN Radio.
