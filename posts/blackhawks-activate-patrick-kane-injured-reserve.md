Patrick Kane is back. The Chicago Blackhawks announced Nov. 12 that the forward has come off injured reserve, and he will play tonight when Chicago hosts the Buffalo Sabres at 7 p.m. at United Center.

The team listed Kane's injury as an upper-body issue.

Kane, 37, has one goal and eight assists for nine points in 15 games with the Blackhawks in 2026-27, the team said. His eight assists rank second on the club, and his nine points rank third.

Fans can watch the game in-market on CHSN or out-of-market on ESPN+, and listen on WGN Radio.
