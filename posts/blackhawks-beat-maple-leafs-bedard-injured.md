The Chicago Blackhawks beat the Toronto Maple Leafs 6-3 at United Center on Tuesday, but they lost Connor Bedard to an apparent upper-body injury 54 seconds into the third period, according to the game recap on NHL.com. Bedard was hurt while taking a face-off.

The injury is the big worry for Chicago (6-13-2). Bedard missed the first six games of the season while recovering from left shoulder surgery in July. Last season he missed 12 games with an upper-body injury, also suffered on a face-off, in a game at St. Louis on Dec. 12.

Coach Jeff Blashill said Bedard will get more testing. "He's going to get some further testing here," Blashill said. He hopes to have an update Wednesday, but he declined to say more until he knows more.

The team kept winning without him. Tyler Bertuzzi had two goals and an assist, including a power-play goal at 9:07 of the third that made it 4-2. Frank Nazar scored twice in the second period and now has five goals during a three-game goal streak.

"Guys need to step up, and that was a huge thing for all of us," Nazar said of the mood after Bedard went down.

Sam Rinzel had three assists for his first three-point game in the NHL. Spencer Knight made 31 saves. Oliver Moore also scored, making it 5-3 at 11:21 of the third.

Bedard scored too, a wrist shot from the left dot at 18:51 of the second that restored Chicago's lead. It came right after officials ruled that Toronto's Teddy Blueger had scored at 18:16, wiping out an apparent Moore goal. Moore called Bedard's goal "absolutely massive after that."

Toronto (8-13-2) has lost seven straight, the first six at home. Easton Cowan opened the scoring and John Tavares also scored, and Sergei Bobrovsky made 19 saves. Maple Leafs coach Jim Hiller said he was disappointed in how his team played the second half of the game.

The night also marked the 100th anniversary of the Blackhawks' first NHL game, a 4-1 win over the Toronto St. Patricks at Chicago Coliseum on Nov. 17, 1926.
