The Chicago Blackhawks placed captain Connor Bedard on injured reserve with a shoulder injury, the team announced Nov. 22. The move is retroactive to Nov. 17. The club also recalled forward Dominic Toninato from the Rockford IceHogs of the AHL.

This matters because Bedard leads Chicago in scoring. The 21-year-old has 18 points (7 goals, 11 assists) in 15 games this season, which is the most on the team. He is third on the Blackhawks in both goals and assists, and he has picked up at least one point in 13 of his 15 games.

The team did not give a timeline for his return in the announcement.

Toninato, 32, has played three NHL games for Chicago this season. He has three hits and has won 10 of 18 faceoffs (55.6%).

Down in the AHL, the Duluth, Minn., native has 11 points (4 goals, 7 assists) in 14 games with Rockford. He is tied for third on the IceHogs in goals, fifth in assists and points, and third with two power play goals.

Chicago plays Vancouver on Sunday at 7 p.m. at Rogers Arena.
