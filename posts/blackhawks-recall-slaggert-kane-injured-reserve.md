The Chicago Blackhawks recalled forward Landon Slaggert from the AHL's Rockford IceHogs and placed forward Patrick Kane on injured reserve with a lower-body injury, the team announced Friday. Kane's IR move is retroactive to Nov. 25.

The 38-year-old Kane has 18 points (2 goals, 16 assists) in 22 games. He shares the team lead in assists with 16 and is tied for second on the club in points.

Slaggert, 24, steps in after putting up 10 points (4 goals, 6 assists) in 20 games for Rockford in 2026-27. He shares the IceHogs' lead in game-winning goals with two, is tied for third on the team in goals, and ranks sixth in both points and assists.

The South Bend, Ind. native has played 102 regular-season NHL games, all with Chicago, and has 17 points (6 goals, 11 assists). He set career highs in 2025-26 with 53 games played, three goals and seven points. He also matched his best mark in assists with four.

The roster move comes on game day. The Blackhawks host the New York Rangers at 4:30 p.m. CT at United Center. The game airs nationally on TNT and on WGN radio.
