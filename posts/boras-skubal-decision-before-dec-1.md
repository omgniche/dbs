Tarik Skubal's free-agent decision may be close. His agent, Scott Boras, said on MLB Network Radio's The Front Office that there is a "strong likelihood" the lefty picks a team before the current Collective Bargaining Agreement expires at 11:59 p.m. ET on Dec. 1.

Skubal is considered the consensus top player available this offseason. Boras said the process, which he has nicknamed the "Skuba-l dive," is "deep into the process."

Boras would not discuss specific offers. Early reports had the Mets offering nearly $400 million over nine years, but he declined to comment on negotiations with any club.

"I can tell you that we've completed all of our meetings with Tarik and the teams of interest, [the teams] that he has interest in," Boras said. "The negotiations have been ongoing here for the past few days."

The teams reported to have met with Skubal are the Mets, who many consider the favorite, the Dodgers, the Padres, the Blue Jays and at least one of two possible mystery teams. The Dodgers are his incumbent club.

"I think it's pretty clear that we're certainly going to, more than likely, make a decision before Dec. 1," Boras added. "We have enough information."

## What it means for the rest of the market

Skubal's choice will also affect other teams. Boras said it could influence the decisions of "10-15" other teams, since attention would then shift to the next group of free-agent pitchers. That group includes Michael King, Freddy Peralta, Trevor Rogers and Kaima Taira.
