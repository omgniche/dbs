Brandon Aiyuk met with the San Francisco 49ers for about an hour Friday afternoon as he tries to get reinstated to the team's roster. He arrived at the team's business offices in Santa Clara with new agent Drew Rosenhaus.

The meeting comes with a financial hurdle. A person with knowledge of the situation told the AP that the league sent Aiyuk and the NFL Players Association a letter Thursday saying he must repay the 49ers about $13 million in bonuses. Those bonuses were paid after he signed a four-year, $120 million extension just before the 2024 season. The person spoke anonymously because the letter wasn't made public. The Ringer first reported that the team wanted the money back.

Aiyuk did not address the money when he arrived. He said he's healthy after his knee injury.

"I’m feeling great, feeling healthy. Long recovery process, long rehab process," Aiyuk said. "But I’m back to 100 percent and looking to get reinstated into the National Football League."

Rosenhaus declined to share details from the meeting and said, "To be continued." He also wouldn't talk about where Aiyuk might play.

The 49ers' side sounded far less eager. Coach Kyle Shanahan said he had no role in the meeting and called the situation no distraction. "It’s been over for our football team for a long time," Shanahan said.

General manager John Lynch told KNBR on Thursday that the team is working with the NFL management council on what he called a "unique" situation. "It’s fluid," Lynch said, adding that the team will "look for a pathway for both sides here that works." Lynch has previously said he doesn't expect Aiyuk to play for the Niners again.

## Where things stand

Aiyuk hasn't played in more than two years. He went down with a knee injury in October 2024 and has been on the reserve/left squad list since leaving the team late last season. He fired agent Ryan Williams during the dispute and went without representation until he hired Rosenhaus.

He will remain under contract with San Francisco once the NFL reinstates him. The 49ers previously voided $27 million in guaranteed money in his 2026 contract after he skipped meetings and other team activities last summer while rehabbing.

Aiyuk is 28. He had 75 catches for 1,342 yards and seven touchdowns in 2023 and made second-team All-Pro. He then had 25 catches for 374 yards in seven games in 2024 before the injury. Since the 49ers drafted him in the first round in 2020, he has 294 catches for 4,305 yards and 25 touchdowns.
