Devils defenseman Brenden Dillon has been suspended four games without pay for an illegal check to the head of Columbus Blue Jackets defenseman Denton Mateychuk, the NHL Department of Player Safety announced Nov. 26.

The hit came Wednesday, Nov. 25, in Columbus, at 12:48 of the first period. Dillon was given a match penalty on the play.

Under the Collective Bargaining Agreement, and based on his average annual salary, Dillon will forfeit $82,474.24. The league said the money goes to the Players' Emergency Assistance Fund.
