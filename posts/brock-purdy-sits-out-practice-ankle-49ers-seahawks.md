San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy did not practice Wednesday because of an ankle injury, coach Kyle Shanahan said. Purdy got hurt in the second quarter of Sunday's win over the Minnesota Vikings.

Shanahan said it is not a high ankle sprain and that Purdy is day to day. He also said there is a "possibility" Purdy won't be able to play Sunday against the Seattle Seahawks.

This matters because Purdy is the MVP frontrunner and the 49ers are 9-1. Seattle is also the opponent that could help San Francisco close out the division. The Niners already lead the NFC West by three games, and a win Sunday could be the knockout blow in the race.

There is some good news. Purdy was able to finish Sunday's game, and the injury is a low ankle issue rather than a high one. Still, San Francisco won't put its quarterback at risk when the long-term goals are at stake.

If Purdy can't go, Mac Jones will start in his place. The picture should get clearer after Thursday's and Friday's practices.

Purdy wasn't the only player held out Wednesday. Mike Evans (Achilles), Dre Greenlaw (neck), Siran Neal (knee) and Jordan Watkins (groin) also did not practice.
