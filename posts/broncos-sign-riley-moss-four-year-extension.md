The Denver Broncos have locked up cornerback Riley Moss. The team announced Thursday that it signed him to a four-year contract extension that runs through the 2030 season.

For Broncos fans, this keeps a steady starter in the secondary. Moss, a 2023 third-round pick, has started all 39 games he has played over the last three seasons, and his coverage numbers have been strong.

In 2025, Moss set a career high with 19 passes defensed, which tied for the most in the league. He also faced an NFL-high 118 targets that year. Even so, he allowed a 57.6 percent completion rate, the sixth lowest among players who were targeted at least 80 times, according to Pro Football Reference.

This season has gone even better. Through eight games, quarterbacks are completing just 51.4 percent of their passes against him. In four of the seven qualifying games he has played in 2026, he has held opposing quarterbacks under a 70.0 passer rating when they throw his way. In Week 2, Jacksonville's Trevor Lawrence had a 1.6 passer rating against him.

Moss also made a big play in the Broncos' home-opening win over the Jaguars. His interception turned the momentum and helped spark a comeback victory.

Since the start of 2025, he has three games with at least three passes defensed. That ties him with two other players for the most in the NFL over that stretch.

In 53 regular-season games, Moss has three interceptions, 35 passes defensed, a forced fumble, a fumble recovery, a sack, 199 tackles, four tackles for loss and a quarterback hit.
