Deshaun Watson will start at quarterback for the Cleveland Browns on Sunday against the Texans, the team announced Wednesday.

The decision settles a question that was still open earlier this week. As of Monday, coach Todd Monken had not said who would start in Week 10.

For Browns fans, it means Watson is back under center after a week on the sideline. He cleared concussion protocol before Cleveland's Week 9 game but was inactive, and Shedeur Sanders got the start in the loss to New Orleans. The Browns are 5-4.

## Other moves around the league

The Atlanta Falcons (3-6) signed kicker Jake Moody and cut kicker Nick Folk, according to NFL.com's transactions roundup. Atlanta also activated offensive lineman Cameron Williams, who had been on injured reserve with an ankle injury.

The Kansas City Chiefs (6-2) released wide receiver Nikko Remigio and defensive end Jack Pyburn.
