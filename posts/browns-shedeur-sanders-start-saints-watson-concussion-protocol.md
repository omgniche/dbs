Shedeur Sanders will start at quarterback for the Browns on Sunday against the Saints. It will be his first start of the 2026 season. Deshaun Watson remains in concussion protocol.

The timing matters for Cleveland (5-3), which has slipped to third in the AFC North. The Steelers (6-2) lead the division and the Bengals (5-2) sit second. The Browns also come off a 27-6 loss in Pittsburgh in which they piled up 319 yards but scored only six points.

Coach Todd Monken said he hasn't decided whether Watson would be active if he clears protocol by Sunday. "He's still not able to do team reps," Monken said. He added that Watson "can do individual, he can do some routes, been in meetings, but you're not able to do any of the team reps, which makes it difficult."

## What Sanders brings

Sanders, 24, was a fifth-round pick out of Colorado in 2025. He took over as Cleveland's starter in the second half of that season and appeared in eight games, seven as a starter. He went 3-4 and completed 56.6% of his passes for 1,400 yards with seven touchdowns and 10 interceptions.

His rookie year left plenty of questions about whether he could be the long-term answer. He lost the preseason quarterback competition to Watson. This season, Sanders has made two appearances and completed all three of his pass attempts for 2 yards.

Dillon Gabriel, a 2025 third-round pick, will back up Sanders.

## Watson's season so far

Watson has played well in his first action since the season-ending injury in 2024. He has put up back-to-back 300-yard games and has five game-winning drives, tied for the most in the league. He has also taken a league-high 33 sacks.

The concussion means Watson will miss at least one game in every season he has been with the Browns, who acquired him from the Texans ahead of 2022. His eight starts this year are already a single-season high for him in Cleveland.

Kickoff in New Orleans is at 1 p.m. ET on Sunday.
