The Montreal Canadiens have acquired forward Dylan Larkin from the Detroit Red Wings, general manager Kent Hughes announced Sunday, according to a team news release.

Montreal is sending Detroit forward Kirby Dach, goaltender Samuel Montembeault, the rights to forward Michael Hage, a 2027 first-round pick, a 2027 second-round pick, a 2028 fourth-round pick and a 2029 first-round pick. The Canadiens also get back Detroit's 2028 second-round pick and its 2027 third-round pick.

For Canadiens fans, the headline is the kind of center coming in. Larkin, 30, has scored at least 30 goals in each of the past five seasons, and six times in his career, per the team. He is a left-handed center.

The price is a big one. Two players go out, along with Hage's rights and a long list of draft picks, including first-rounders in 2027 and 2029.

## What Larkin brings

Larkin has six points (two goals, four assists) in 10 games with Detroit this season, with a plus-3 rating. He led Red Wings forwards in average ice time at 21:41, the team said.

He debuted in the NHL in 2015 and has 278 goals and 649 points in 818 games, all with Detroit. That ranks tenth in Red Wings history in both goals and points.

Larkin also has international experience. He helped the United States win gold at the 2026 Milano-Cortina Olympics and played for his country at the 4 Nations Face-Off in 2025.
