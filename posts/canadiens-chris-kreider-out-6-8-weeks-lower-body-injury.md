Chris Kreider will miss six to eight weeks with a lower-body injury, the Montreal Canadiens announced Saturday.

The forward got hurt in Thursday's 5-2 win over the Boston Bruins. He has nine points (three goals, six assists) in 19 games this season. The Canadiens are 12-3-4.

Kreider left the game at 6:27 of the third period after an open-ice collision with Bruins forward Michael Eyssimont. He lay briefly on the ice before play was stopped, then got up and hopped to the locker room without putting weight on his left leg, according to NHL.com.

Teammate Jake Evans spoke about Kreider's impact in the room. "He's a true pro and a great teammate," Evans said. He added that "he's going to come back strong."

Montreal is back in action Saturday night at 7 p.m. ET, when it hosts the Colorado Avalanche.
