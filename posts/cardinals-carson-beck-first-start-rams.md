Carson Beck will make his first NFL start Sunday when the Arizona Cardinals face the Los Angeles Rams, coach Mike LaFleur announced Wednesday.

The third-round rookie steps in for Gardner Minshew, who landed on injured reserve this week with an elbow injury. Minshew got hurt in Arizona's 17-3 loss at Seattle in Week 9. A week earlier, he had come off the bench mid-game to spark a comeback win, and the Cardinals then traded previous starter Jacoby Brissett to the Ravens.

For fans, this is the first real look at a quarterback who has been buried on the depth chart all year. At 3-6, Arizona now gets to see what it has in the former Miami star.

There isn't much NFL tape on Beck. His best is from the Hall of Fame Game in early August, when he went 15 of 19 for 188 yards and a touchdown. A rib injury then cost him the rest of the preseason.

Before the NFL, Beck was a four-star recruit at Georgia and lost the starting job battle to Gunner Stockton. He transferred to Miami in 2025 and led the Hurricanes to the College Football Playoff National Championship Game, with wins over Notre Dame, Florida State, Pittsburgh, Ohio State and Ole Miss along the way.

The Cardinals say they like what they've seen in practice. "I know this isn't too big for him," LaFleur said, per ESPN.com. "Go have some fun, man. Go let it sling."

LaFleur also said, "You never really know until you get to the live bullets on Sunday, but just the way he goes about his business ... have all the confidence that he's ready to roll."

Quarterbacks coach Matt Schaub noted Beck lost some reps and development time because of the injury in camp. Schaub said Beck's workload has ramped back up and "we like where he's at."
