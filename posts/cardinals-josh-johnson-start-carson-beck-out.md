The Arizona Cardinals announced Saturday that Josh Johnson will start at quarterback Sunday against the Chiefs. Carson Beck has been downgraded to out and will be inactive with back spasms, the team said.

For Cardinals fans, it's another new name under center. Johnson will be the fourth different Arizona starting quarterback in four weeks. Jacoby Brissett started in Dallas, Gardner Minshew started in Seattle, and Beck got the nod last week at home against the Rams.

The move was no surprise. Coach Mike LaFleur said Friday that the team was preparing as if Johnson would start. Rookie Joey Aguilar will back him up.

LaFleur kept the focus on the basics. "There is no next step to the playbook, this is going back to the starting table of contents, and that's OK," he said.

Johnson is a veteran with plenty of miles. Sunday will be his 12th career start and his 51st NFL appearance, a run that began when he first entered the league in 2009.
