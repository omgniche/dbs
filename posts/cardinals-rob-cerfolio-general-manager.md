The St. Louis Cardinals are set to promote 34-year-old Rob Cerfolio to general manager, MLB.com's Mark Feinsand reported, citing a source. The club has not confirmed the move.

Cerfolio has been an assistant GM with St. Louis since 2024. He would continue to work under president of baseball operations Chaim Bloom.

The news follows longtime Cardinals executive Randy Flores becoming the Astros' general manager this past week. For fans, it means one of the team's rising young front-office figures is staying in St. Louis.

Cerfolio was reportedly a person of interest for several open front-office jobs this offseason. One was the Athletics' top baseball job, which went to Taylor Smith.

Before joining the Cardinals, Cerfolio spent 2015-24 in the Guardians' front office and rose to director of player development. He is a Yale graduate.

## A hand in the youth movement

Cerfolio played a key role in overseeing the Cardinals' youth movement in 2026. Jordan Walker and JJ Wetherholt had major breakouts, and Joshua Báez and Leo Bernal made their debuts.

The farm system is still strong looking toward 2027 and beyond. Catcher Rainiel Rodriguez leads it and ranks as MLB Pipeline's No. 10 overall prospect.
