DJ Uiagalelei will start at quarterback for the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday against the Patriots. Head coach Jim Harbaugh announced the decision Friday after Justin Herbert was ruled out of the game with a concussion.

It will be the first NFL start for Uiagalelei, an undrafted free agent who has worked his way up from the practice squad. Chargers fans will get a look at a 25-year-old who has been in the building since 2025 and has only one regular-season appearance so far.

Harbaugh said he's glad to see him get the chance. "I'm excited for him. He's prepared all season long like every week was going to be the opportunity," Harbaugh said Friday. He also called Uiagalelei "very talented" and said that "getting all the reps this week has really helped."

Uiagalelei said he's thankful. "Playing for the Chargers, playing for an unbelievable organization, unbelievable coaching staff from top to bottom to the Spanos Family. Just thank them for the opportunity," he said.

## What to know about Uiagalelei

His regular-season debut came in Week 9 against the Texans. He entered with about eight minutes left in the fourth quarter and completed his first NFL pass to Keaton Mitchell. Facing fourth-and-13 in the red zone, he then threw a touchdown to Derius Davis for his first career scoring pass.

He signed with the Chargers after the 2025 NFL Draft and played in three of the team's four preseason games that year. He spent the 2025 season on the practice squad, then returned this year on a reserve/future deal and competed for the backup job.

He has also spoken about learning from Herbert. "He's an unbelievable person, unbelievable teammate and I couldn't ask for a better quarterback to be around to watch," Uiagalelei said.

In college, he played 53 games at Clemson, Oregon State and Florida State. He threw for 9,384 yards and 61 touchdowns, with 30 interceptions, and added 21 rushing scores. His best season came at Oregon State, where he led the team to eight wins, threw for 2,638 yards and 21 touchdowns and was a Davey O'Brien Award semifinalist.

He is also a Southern California product. He starred at St. John Bosco in Bellflower, where he led the team to a 7A state title and a No. 1 national ranking in his senior year.
