Justin Herbert will not play Sunday when the Chargers face the Patriots in Week 12. The quarterback is still in concussion protocol, so second-year QB DJ Uiagalelei will make his first career start.

This is a big change for a Chargers team that sits at 1-9, the worst record in the league. Herbert is having the worst season of his pro career, and the offense now turns to an unproven passer as it looks for its second win. Kickoff is 4:05 p.m. ET at SoFi Stadium.

Herbert entered the protocol after playing all of the Week 11 loss to the Jets. He went 15-of-21 for 131 yards and two touchdowns and also ran for a score. It will be his first game missed because of injury since 2023, when a broken finger kept him out for the final four games.

Through 10 games this season, Herbert has completed a career-low 62.4% of his passes for 1,851 yards, eight touchdowns and 11 interceptions. He has also been sacked a league-high 39 times, on 12.46% of his dropbacks. The Chargers' scoring offense ranks 31st.

## Who is Uiagalelei?

Uiagalelei went undrafted in 2025 after a college career from 2020 to 2024 that took him through Clemson, Oregon State and Florida State. This season he has appeared in one game, finishing 2-of-7 for 24 yards and a touchdown in mop-up duty against Houston.

Jake Browning will back him up. Los Angeles signed Browning to the active roster earlier this week.
