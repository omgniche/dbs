The Kansas City Chiefs acquired wide receiver Josh Downs from the Indianapolis Colts on Tuesday.

For fans, this is a new weapon for the passing game. Downs' calling card is his ability to win against man coverage, and that skillset should help open things up for Rashee Rice, Xavier Worthy and the other receivers.

Downs is 25 and had played his whole career in Indianapolis. He has 231 catches, 2,494 yards and 12 touchdowns as a Colt. He played at least 14 games in each of the last three seasons and had no fewer than 55 catches and 550 yards in any of them. His best year was 2024, with 72 catches for 803 yards and five touchdowns. This season he has 33 catches for 354 yards and a score in nine games.

Chiefs fans may remember him. In Week 2, Downs had his best game of the year against Kansas City on Sunday Night Football, catching seven passes for 72 yards. Six of those catches moved the chains.

## Why the Chiefs wanted him

According to Pro Football Focus, Downs has been targeted 19 times against man coverage this season, the seventh-most in the league. He turned those looks into 11 catches, 126 yards, nine first downs and 63 yards after the catch.

He has also been a third-down option. His 10 third-down catches for a first down rank 12th in the NFL, even with a Colts offense that has struggled to throw the ball lately.

## Background

Downs was the No. 79 overall pick in the 2023 draft, and he was the 12th receiver taken. At North Carolina, he set school single-season records in 2021 with 101 catches and 1,335 yards. He left Chapel Hill third in school history in receptions (202) and fifth in receiving yards (2,483).

Football runs in the family. His younger brother, Caleb, went No. 11 overall in the 2026 draft, and his uncle, Dre' Bly, played 11 years in the NFL.
