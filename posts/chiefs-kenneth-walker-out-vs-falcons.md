The Chiefs will play Sunday's game against the Falcons without running back Kenneth Walker III. He was downgraded to out with a groin injury, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport and ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Saturday.

Walker suffered a minor groin strain in Thursday's practice and then sat out Friday's session. The reports say Kansas City is being cautious by holding him out.

This is a big loss for the Chiefs' offense. Walker is in his first season with the team and has been its engine, with 872 rushing yards, 127 receiving yards and 12 total touchdowns through eight games. His 109.0 rushing yards per game leads the league.

Rookie Emmett Johnson will lead the rushing attack for the first time on Sunday. He has run for 111 yards and a touchdown on 4.3 yards per carry this season. He has also caught 14 passes for 237 yards and another score.

Fellow rookie Jaydn Ott and second-year back Nate Carter will back him up. Carter was elevated from the practice squad.

Patrick Mahomes could also throw more against Atlanta, which might mean more targets for wide receiver Josh Downs. Downs is a recent addition and will play his first game as a Chief.

Kickoff between the Chiefs and Falcons is set for 1 p.m. ET on Sunday.
