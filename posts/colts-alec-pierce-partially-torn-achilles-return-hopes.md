Indianapolis Colts wide receiver Alec Pierce said Wednesday that he partially tore his left Achilles in the Week 2 loss to the Kansas City Chiefs. It was his first time speaking to reporters since the injury.

Pierce said the problem is where the tendon attaches to his heel, not the tendon itself. "I injured my Achilles, but I didn't rupture it," he said, calling that the silver lining. He added that the injury "could've been a lot worse."

## Why it matters

The Colts are 3-8 and appear headed for another missed postseason, but Pierce said there is still "a chance" he plays again this year. He has recently started jogging and doing plyometrics as he works through rehab.

Pierce has been out since Week 2. He led the NFL in yards per reception in 2024 and 2025 and missed only four games in his first four seasons.

## How the heel issue got here

Pierce said he had a PRP injection in his left heel at the end of the 2025 season to ease discomfort he had been managing since 2024. When that didn't work, he had surgery in March, which didn't fully fix things either. He said he tried to play through pain early this season before further hurting the area in the Chiefs game.

He did not have a second surgery, but he did get another injection in the heel, one he described as "different" from the PRP shot. He didn't say what type. Pierce said his doctor told him at his latest checkup that the heel is "going to heal completely fine."

The Colts re-signed Pierce to a four-year, $114 million contract in March, shortly before the surgery. According to the report, it was the richest free-agent deal for a receiver in NFL history.

## A team in upheaval

Pierce said it has been frustrating to watch the team struggle without him. The Colts have fired general manager Chris Ballard and cut quarterback Daniel Jones, and traded Quenton Nelson, DeForest Buckner, Josh Downs and Keenan Allen for draft capital. Anthony Richardson Sr. will start Sunday against the New York Giants.

Pierce said Jones' exit was hard on him. "I've just felt like I've let some people down," he said. He also said the roster moves give him a chance to "step up into a leadership role."
