The Colts are shutting down wide receiver Alec Pierce for the rest of the 2026 season, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported Sunday. The plan is for Pierce to be fully healthy for 2027.

Pierce has been on injured reserve with a heel injury since Week 2. Indianapolis reportedly turned down his push to come back this year, and Rapoport said Pierce has worked hard in rehab but the team wants to avoid another setback.

## Why it matters

Pierce is expected to be the Colts' top receiving target next year, alongside running back Jonathan Taylor and tight end Tyler Warren. He is also one of the few building blocks left on offense for whoever becomes the next general manager.

It has been a rough year in Indianapolis. The Colts are 3-8 with six games to play after a blowout loss to the Texans on Thursday night, and they have no playoff hopes. GM Chris Ballard was fired in October. The team traded away receivers Josh Downs and Keenan Allen, plus Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner, before the deadline. It then waived quarterback Daniel Jones.

## The injury timeline

Pierce signed a $114 million contract to stay with the Colts before free agency began. He then had ankle/heel surgery and aimed to be back for training camp. He came off the physically unable to perform list on Aug. 27 and played in the first two games, catching five passes for 72 yards on 49 snaps. He aggravated the heel in the Week 2 loss and went on IR.

Pierce was a second-round pick in 2022. He has 162 catches for 3,006 yards and 17 touchdowns in 66 games. He led the NFL in yards per reception in both 2024 (21.3) and 2025 (22.3).
