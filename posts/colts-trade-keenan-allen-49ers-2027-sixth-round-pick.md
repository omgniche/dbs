The Indianapolis Colts traded wide receiver Keenan Allen to the San Francisco 49ers on Tuesday in exchange for a 2027 sixth-round draft pick, the team announced. The deal was done ahead of the 4 p.m. trade deadline.

Allen signed as a free agent in August and played eight games for the Colts in 2026, catching 40 passes on 58 targets for 351 yards and one touchdown. He leaves with 1,095 career receptions, eighth most in NFL history.

It's the third trade the Colts have made since Week 1 of the 2026 season, and the second with San Francisco. Last month, Indianapolis sent defensive tackle DeForest Buckner to the 49ers for a 2027 fourth-round pick.

## Where the Colts' picks stand

According to the team, the Colts now hold nine picks in the 2027 draft:

- Second round
- Third round
- Third round (from Seattle in the Quenton Nelson trade)
- Fourth round
- Fourth round (from San Francisco in the Buckner trade)
- Fifth round
- Sixth round (from San Francisco in the Allen trade)
- Seventh round
- Seventh round (from Buffalo in the Sedrick Van Pran-Granger trade)

In 2028, they have a pick in every round, plus an extra fifth-rounder from Seattle in the Nelson deal.
