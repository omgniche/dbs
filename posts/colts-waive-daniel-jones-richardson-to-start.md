Colts head coach Shane Steichen said Friday that the team waived quarterback Daniel Jones and will start Anthony Richardson Sr. in Week 12 against the New York Giants next weekend. Steichen said the move was "made for the best interests of both parties." Jones did not ask to be released, he said.

For fans, this is a big shake-up for a season that has gone sideways. Indianapolis is 3-8 and has lost six of its last seven, three of those to AFC South opponents. Steichen pointed to "where we're at right now in the season" as the reason for the switch.

The change came after a 26-3 loss to the Texans on Thursday. That followed a Week 6 loss to the previously winless Titans, after which general manager Chris Ballard was dismissed. The Colts also lost to the Jaguars and Texans in Weeks 8 and 11 by a combined 58 points.

Jones finished his Colts run last in the NFL with a 65.4 passer rating and a league-high 13 interceptions. Steichen didn't put it all on him, though. "I don't think it was just on one person," he said. "Obviously, the quarterback takes a lot of the blame. That's the business that we're in."

Steichen praised Jones's toughness. Jones played several games last season on a broken leg, then tore his Achilles in Week 14. He re-signed in March and took part in every training camp practice. "Can't say enough good things about Daniel. Just the ultimate competitor, ultimate teammate," Steichen said.

## Richardson gets the job

Richardson, the No. 4 overall pick in 2023, will make his first start since Week 16 of the 2024 season. He has been Jones's backup for the last two years and has been praised for how he handled that role.

"From his rookie year to now, obviously he's matured a lot with his preparation and how he goes about it," Steichen said. He added that Richardson "brings a unique skillset that's very athletic and can run around and make some plays."

Steichen said "there's a world" in which Riley Leonard, the team's third-string quarterback, also plays. "We'll evaluate that each and every week throughout the season," he said.

The offense is averaging 19.2 points per game, seventh-lowest in the league. "We've got to get that thing back on track in a hurry," Steichen said, noting six games remain.

Colts.com reported Steichen's comments.
