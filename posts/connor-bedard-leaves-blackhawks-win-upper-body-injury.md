Chicago Blackhawks captain Connor Bedard left Tuesday's 6-3 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs at United Center with an upper-body injury, NHL.com reported.

Bedard was lining up for a face-off against Toronto's Nick Paul 54 seconds into the third period when Paul's stick hit his right hand. He yelled out in pain and went straight to the locker room.

The 21-year-old leads the team with 18 points (seven goals, 11 assists) in 15 games. He scored his seventh goal of the season at 18:51 of the second period, shortly before the injury. The source gave no update on his condition or how long he might be out.

## A tough run with injuries

This is the latest health scare for Bedard. He hurt his left shoulder in an offseason training session in Vancouver on July 2 and had surgery a few days later. He was first expected to be out until November, but he returned Oct. 13 in a 5-3 win over the Pittsburgh Penguins.

He also hurt his right shoulder on a last-second face-off against the St. Louis Blues on Dec. 12, 2025. He returned Jan. 9 against the Washington Capitals, but he did not take face-offs in a game until Feb. 26 against the Nashville Predators, when he won 8 of 16.

As a rookie, Bedard missed about six weeks with a fractured jaw suffered against the New Jersey Devils on Jan. 5, 2024.

The No. 1 pick in the 2023 NHL Draft has 221 points (82 goals, 139 assists) in 234 games with Chicago.
