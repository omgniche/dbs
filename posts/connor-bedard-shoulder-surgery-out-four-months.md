Chicago Blackhawks captain Connor Bedard had successful surgery on his right shoulder Monday and should make a full recovery in about four months, team physician Dr. Michael Terry said in a statement, NHL.com reported.

The 21-year-old center got hurt in Chicago's 6-3 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs on Nov. 17. He was setting up for a face-off against Nick Paul 54 seconds into the third period when Paul's stick hit Bedard's right hand as Bedard put his stick down. Bedard yelled out in pain and went straight to the locker room.

Bedard has 18 points (seven goals, 11 assists) in 15 games this season, tied with Roman Kantserov for the team lead. For his career, the No. 1 pick in the 2023 draft has 221 points in 234 games with Chicago.

## A third shoulder injury in under a year

This is Bedard's third shoulder injury in less than a year. He hurt his left shoulder in an offseason workout in Vancouver on July 2 and had surgery a few days later. He was first expected back in November but returned Oct. 13.

His first right-shoulder injury happened almost the same way, on a last-second face-off against the St. Louis Blues on Dec. 12, 2025. He returned Jan. 9 against the Washington Capitals but did not take face-offs in a game until Feb. 26 against Nashville.

Coach Jeff Blashill said Wednesday that in both cases Bedard "actually hit the opponent's stick on the way down." On whether face-offs are now a concern, Blashill said: "We knew there was high risk of it coming back out, not just (on) face-offs, just high risk." He added that "part of what he is as a centerman is taking face-offs," and that the team will look at ways to take them "without picking your stick up and coming down."

Forward Patrick Kane said Thursday: "Obviously we're thinking about him." He added that the team wants to let Bedard know "we're here for him."

The Blackhawks are 8-14-2 and have won two straight. They wrap up a three-game road trip Wednesday in Seattle against the Kraken.
