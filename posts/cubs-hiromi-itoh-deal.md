Right-hander Hiromi Itoh has agreed to a deal with the Cubs, according to a source who spoke to MLB.com's Mark Feinsand on Friday night. Chicago has not confirmed the signing.

Itoh is the second posted NPB star headed to the Cubs. Teruaki Sato is also joining Chicago, and Kaima Taira has landed with the Blue Jays, so all three of the big names posted from Japan this offseason now have deals.

Itoh was posted by the Hokkaido Nippon-Ham Fighters and ranks among the most decorated starters in Japan. He won the Sawamura Award, the NPB's version of the Cy Young, in 2025 after posting a 2.52 ERA with 195 strikeouts in 196 2/3 innings.

He slipped a bit in 2026 but was still very good. Itoh posted a 3.18 ERA with 159 strikeouts in 172 2/3 innings over 26 starts, and he walked just 4.7 percent of batters. Across his career with the Fighters, which began in 2021, he has thrown just over 1,000 innings with 902 strikeouts and a 2.92 ERA.

## What to expect

Itoh is listed at 5-foot-9, which is considered small for an MLB starter. Yakyu Cosmopolitan wrote that he has more than 10 different pitch grips and shapes, which gives him a deep arsenal.

A scout told MLB.com's Michael Clair that the role could be a question. "He could be an up and down starter," the scout said. "But maybe in relief he might show some more velocity. In the previous WBC, he came out of the bullpen and he was 94-96 with some nasty stuff."

The contract terms were not part of the report.
