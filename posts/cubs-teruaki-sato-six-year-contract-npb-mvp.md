Teruaki Sato has a new MLB home. The Cubs and the 27-year-old slugger agreed to a six-year, $120 million contract around 10 a.m. ET Tuesday, sources told MLB.com's Mark Feinsand.

About 18 hours later, Sato was named the Central League MVP in Japan for the second straight year. He got 213 first-place votes. Hanshin Tigers teammate Haruto Takahashi, the Sawamura Award winner, was second with 34.

Cubs fans are getting a proven bat. Sato won the Central League Triple Crown this season, hitting .306 with 39 home runs and 106 RBIs. He added 40 doubles and 68 walks, with a 1.017 OPS over 143 games, MLB.com reported.

He was just as dominant in 2025, when he won his first MVP. That year he hit 40 homers with a .991 OPS. He also began that year by homering off Dodgers lefty Blake Snell in a Tokyo Series exhibition game at the Tokyo Dome.

## Where he fits in Chicago

Sato mostly played third base for Hanshin. According to MLB.com, he is expected to play a corner outfield spot for the Cubs. That would help cover for the possible exits of Seiya Suzuki and Ian Happ, longtime starters who are both free agents. Sato will probably get time at designated hitter too.

It was a busy week on the North Side. Late Monday night, the Cubs also agreed to a five-year deal with left-handed starter Trevor Rogers, per Feinsand's sources.
