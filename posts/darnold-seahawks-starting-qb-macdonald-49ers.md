Sam Darnold will start for the Seattle Seahawks against the San Francisco 49ers on Nov. 29 if he's healthy enough to play, coach Mike Macdonald said Monday.

Darnold has missed the past two games with a quad injury and also has a cracked rib. Macdonald said the plan is for him to return to practice next week after the bye. If he gets a full week of work and Seattle's medical staff clears him, he's the guy.

"Sam's our starting quarterback," Macdonald said during his weekly appearance on Seattle Sports 710. "I can't wait for him to be fully healthy, probably for the first time -- minimal times it's happened this year."

## Lock keeps winning

The decision comes after Drew Lock stepped in again Sunday and went 14 of 17 for 240 yards and two touchdowns in a 21-13 win over the Las Vegas Raiders. It was the fourth time this season Lock has filled in for Darnold. Seattle is now 4-0 with him at quarterback: 3-0 as a starter and 1-0 in relief. He lost two fumbles in the Raiders game.

On the season, Lock has completed 73.1 percent of his passes for 837 yards and seven touchdowns, with no interceptions. Macdonald credited his behind-the-scenes preparation over the past two years. "Drew's got a spirit about him, too, where he's got that swag about him," Macdonald said.

Lock is in a contract year. Asked whether he'd want to stay in a backup role with Seattle beyond this season, he dodged the question. "Shoot, I love playing. We'll keep it at that," Lock said.

## Why it matters

The matchup is a big one in the NFC West. The 49ers are 8-1 and hold a 2 1/2-game lead over the 6-4 Seahawks.

Darnold hasn't matched last season's numbers. In seven games, he has completed 60.2 percent of his passes for 1,478 yards, 10 touchdowns and nine interceptions. He also hurt his glute in the season opener and missed Week 2. Last year he threw for 4,048 yards, 25 touchdowns and 14 interceptions, made his second straight Pro Bowl and helped lead Seattle to the Super Bowl title.

"Sam was pulling the trigger for us when we won our championship," Macdonald said, "and I do feel like we're tied together to a certain extent."
