Cowboys cornerback DaRon Bland will miss Dallas' home game against the Tennessee Titans and several games after that. Head coach Brian Schottenheimer gave an update on Monday. Bland hurt an ankle in Sunday's game against the San Francisco 49ers.

For fans, this is another hit to a player who has struggled to stay healthy. With only seven games left in the season, Bland could miss most of the rest of the schedule. His official timeline hasn't been set, though Schottenheimer confirmed he will "miss some time."

Bland had already dealt with an ankle injury from practice last week, which made him a gametime decision against San Francisco. He was cleared to play just before kickoff. Late in the first quarter Sunday, he was helped off the field and into the locker room, and he never came back.

Schottenheimer said Monday that this was the other ankle. He explained how it happened: Bland had worked out before the game and looked great, but he got caught between an offensive lineman and the ground as the 49ers ran a screen pass for a touchdown.

## A tough run of injuries

Bland has had trouble getting back to the All-Pro form that earned him the nickname "Pick-Six King" around the NFL. A stress fracture in his foot right before the 2024 season cost him 11 games that year. A foot injury in 2025 kept him out for two early games.

Another foot issue in December of that 2025 season led the Cowboys to put him on season-ending injured reserve. Now he'll once again be working to get back on the field for Dallas' defense.
