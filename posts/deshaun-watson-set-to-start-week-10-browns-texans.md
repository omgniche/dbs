Deshaun Watson is set to start at quarterback for the Cleveland Browns in Week 10 against the Texans. He was inactive for Week 9 against the Saints after entering the concussion protocol.

For Browns fans, it's good news: the quarterback who led the team to a 5-3 start is back under center, and he returns for a home game. Cleveland plays at Huntington Bank Field after a three-game road stretch.

## How Watson got here

Watson took a helmet-to-helmet hit in the first quarter of Week 8 against the Steelers. He was cleared to return to that game, and cleared again Monday morning after another evaluation. Then he had concussion symptoms the night of Nov. 2 and told the medical staff on Nov. 3.

He entered the protocol Nov. 4 and cleared it Nov. 7. He did not practice Nov. 4 or Nov. 5, then returned in a limited role Nov. 6. Head coach Todd Monken said Watson did individual reps that Friday but no team reps.

## Sanders filled in

Shedeur Sanders started in Watson's place and went 19 of 31 for 297 yards and two touchdowns in a 23-22 overtime loss to the Saints. He hit WR KC Concepcion Jr. for a 64-yard score in the fourth quarter and TE Harold Fannin Jr. for a 39-yard touchdown in overtime.

"I am really proud of him," Monken said of Sanders after the game on Nov. 8.

Through eight games, Watson has completed 163 of 249 passes for 1,806 yards and seven touchdowns. He has thrown three interceptions and been sacked 33 times. He also has 217 rushing yards and a touchdown on 51 carries. Five of Cleveland's games with him included fourth-quarter go-ahead drives.

The Browns begin their Week 10 practice schedule this week.
