The Dodgers are making a push to bring back Tarik Skubal. General manager Brandon Gomes confirmed Tuesday at the GM Meetings in Las Vegas that the team has been in contact with the free-agent lefty's camp, MLB.com reported.

"We've been in touch with his camp, and our expectation is we'll meet at some point," Gomes told reporters.

L.A. acquired the two-time AL Cy Young winner from the Tigers at the Trade Deadline, and he spent the last two and a half months of the season in Dodger blue. He went 5-2 with a 2.61 ERA in 10 regular-season starts for the club. In two postseason starts, he struck out 14 and allowed three runs in 11 innings, a 2.45 ERA. The Dodgers' season ended in the NLCS.

Skubal's year included surgery to remove a loose body from his left elbow, which cost him six weeks of recovery and rehab. He still finished with a 2.72 ERA, 186 strikeouts and 27 walks in 158 2/3 innings. MLB.com notes he could land the largest contract ever for a pitcher in total value and average annual value. Yoshinobu Yamamoto's 12-year deal is currently the biggest in guaranteed dollars for a pitcher.

The Dodgers paid a steep price for the rental, sending top prospects Zyhir Hope (No. 26 on MLB Pipeline's Top 100) and River Ryan (No. 64) to Detroit. Keeping him long term would take a major commitment.

They won't be alone. The Mets are among the other interested teams and have reportedly scheduled a meeting with Skubal for next week.

Skubal chose not to discuss his free agency after the season ended, but he spoke warmly about his time in Los Angeles. Gomes said the feeling is mutual.

"I know that he enjoyed his time here, we loved having him, so I think that's certainly helpful as far as these conversations go," Gomes said. "And outside of that, he's earned the right to be in this position and figure out what's best for him and his family. So we'll have those conversations as things go."
