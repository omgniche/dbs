Luka Dončić sat out the Lakers' 121-116 win over the Detroit Pistons on Sunday because of lower-back tightness, the Associated Press reported.

The Lakers won without their star, which keeps their strong start going. They are 8-2, according to the AP. Dončić is averaging 40.1 points this season and had been on a tear before the absence.

Over his last four games, he is averaging 45.0 points, 9.0 rebounds and 7.8 assists. That stretch includes a 53-point night in Friday's 124-118 win over the Portland Trail Blazers.

The Pistons game was the second one he has missed this year. The first was a 103-87 win over the Los Angeles Clippers on Oct. 28.

The source did not say how long the tightness might keep him out or whether he will play next. The Lakers are back in action Monday night in Atlanta against the Hawks.
