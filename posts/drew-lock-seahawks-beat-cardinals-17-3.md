Drew Lock started for the injured Sam Darnold on Sunday and led the Seattle Seahawks to a 17-3 win over the Cardinals at Lumen Field, Seahawks.com reported. It was Lock's second start of the season.

The win shows how much the Seahawks can lean on their backup quarterback. Lock went 19-of-28 for 175 yards and a touchdown, added 16 rushing yards, and did not turn the ball over or get sacked.

Head coach Mike Macdonald praised his quarterback after the game. "He's a beast. He just threw it around. Just went after it," Macdonald said. He also credited the medical staff for its plan with Lock and gave a shout-out to Jalen Milroe for being ready in case Lock couldn't play or finish.

Lock got limited reps this week because the team was managing a lingering elbow injury. Cooper Kupp said preparing mentally without being able to throw much during the week is really tough to do. "I think he did an unbelievable job and tough through obviously some stuff today as well," Kupp said.

The offensive line made keeping him clean a priority. "Last week, Sam (Darnold) got hit too much," Grey Zabel said. "We put in a point of emphasis to keep the quarterback clean and give him a clean pocket to throw from and Drew (Lock) did an unbelievable job tonight."

The run game helped too. Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price combined for 101 yards on 25 carries. Lock called the running backs the "work horses of this team."

## Lock's oblique injury

Macdonald announced after the game that Lock hurt his oblique during the win, and the extent of the injury is unclear. Lock finished the game anyway.

"I was able to finish with it," Lock said. "But we'll get it looked at and go from there, but not too bad," he said. He added that he went into the tent, had it looked at and came back out, and that it "was nothing that is going to keep me on the sideline."
