Free agent right-hander Dustin May has agreed to a three-year, $52.5 million deal with the White Sox, a source told MLB.com's Mark Feinsand on Tuesday. The White Sox haven't confirmed it. Once it's finalized, May will be the second Brewers free agent to sign elsewhere.

The first was first baseman Jake Bauers, who agreed to a four-year, $58 million deal with the Red Sox. The difference is that Milwaukee made Bauers a qualifying offer, so it will get a Draft pick between the first and second rounds. May got no qualifying offer, so the Brewers get nothing back for him.

May is 29. He became a free agent after turning down his half of a $20 million mutual option, which came with him from St. Louis when the Brewers acquired him and reliever JoJo Romero at the Trade Deadline.

Milwaukee hoped he would bolster the rotation, but he went 2-2 with a 5.65 ERA in 10 starts for the club. Late in September he made a series of shortened outings on short rest, an effort to help him build momentum for October.

In the postseason he worked in a hybrid role. He made two starts and three relief appearances, and allowed seven earned runs in 11 innings. That wasn't enough for the Brewers to risk a qualifying offer, or to bring him back for a rotation that is expected to be led again by Jacob Misiorowski and Logan Henderson in 2027.

## Who fills the rest of the rotation

The Brewers have plenty of young arms for the remaining spots. The list includes postseason star Robert Gasser, left-handers Kyle Harrison and Shane Drohan, and perhaps Colton Gordon.

Right-hander Quinn Priester is also in the mix, provided he recovers from thoracic outlet surgery. So are prospect Coleman Crow, who is No. 23 on the Brewers' list per MLB Pipeline, and bounceback candidate Brandon Sproat.
