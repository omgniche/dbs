Dylan Larkin is a Montreal Canadien, and he was set to play his first game for the team Monday night against the Los Angeles Kings, NHL.com reported. The center flew in from Detroit shortly after 9 a.m. and was on the ice with his new teammates about 90 minutes later.

For Canadiens fans, this is a big addition. Larkin spent his whole career with the Red Wings before Sunday night's trade, and he said he is eager to join a group he called "building toward something special." He also had kind words for coach Marty St. Louis.

Montreal sent goalie Sam Montembeault, forward Kirby Dach and forward prospect Michael Hage to Detroit. Draft picks went both ways in the deal, according to the report. General manager Kent Hughes joined Larkin at his first news conference with the club.

"I'm honored to be here," Larkin said. He added: "Thanks to the Montreal Canadiens for believing in me."

## A familiar chant

Larkin said he knew Montreal fans were interested in him. He heard "We Want Larkin!" chants when the Red Wings visited on Oct. 10. He called the experience "interesting" and said it was "pretty difficult to play" that night. Looking back, he said, it "just shows the passion."

NHL.com noted that a situation in Detroit ended with Larkin suspended in late October. Larkin did not dwell on how he got here. He said he is proud of his time with the Red Wings and thanked the organization, adding, "Maybe it didn't end the way anybody thought it would, but I'm here now."

He will wear No. 89. Larkin said he wore 71 in Detroit but did not want to ask forward Jake Evans, who has that number in Montreal, to give it up. "He's a winning hockey player," Larkin said of Evans.

Larkin said he expects nothing more of himself than to skate hard from his first shift and help the players around him. "Just playing my game -- that's all I can control coming here," he said.

The game against the Kings was set for 7:30 p.m. ET.
