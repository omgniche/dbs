The Eagles activated running back Will Shipley from injured reserve and moved linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks onto IR, the team announced ahead of Sunday's game against the Steelers. Defensive tackle Gabe Hall and linebacker Anfernee Orji were also elevated from the practice squad.

The move costs Philadelphia two key players. Baun leads the Eagles with 65 tackles, eight tackles for loss and two sacks, and both sacks came against the Giants, the game in which he hurt his back before the bye. He did not practice all week. The team says he is tied for first among NFL linebackers in tackles for loss or no gain (9) and forced incompletions (5).

Wicks, acquired in an offseason trade with the Packers, hurt his ankle against the Panthers and has missed the last three games. He returned to practice on a limited basis this week, but a foot injury appeared on Friday's injury report and the Eagles ruled him out. He has 19 catches for 284 yards and a touchdown this season.

Shipley, a third-year back out of Clemson, last played in London against the Jaguars, where he scored his first touchdown of the year. He had been out with a foot injury. Saquon Barkley was a full participant in practice all week with a hamstring injury, after previously dealing with a back injury.

## Practice squad call-ups

Hall (6-6, 295 pounds) has been on the practice squad since his rookie year in 2024. He made the active roster out of training camp in 2025 and played in two games. Sunday will be his 2026 debut, and the team noted he can make a case for a 53-man spot after the trade of Jordan Davis to the Jaguars.

Orji joined the Eagles' practice squad this season after spending all of 2025 on the Titans' injured reserve list. He signed with the Saints as a rookie free agent out of Vanderbilt in 2023, then played 16 games with two starts in 2024, posting 18 tackles, two for loss. If he is active Sunday, he will make his Eagles debut and play his first regular-season NFL game since 2024.
