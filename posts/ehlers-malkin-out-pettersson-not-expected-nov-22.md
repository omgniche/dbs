Hurricanes forward Nikolaj Ehlers will miss Sunday's game at the New York Rangers because of an undisclosed injury, according to NHL.com's status report on Nov. 22. He got hurt in the third period of Carolina's 7-4 win over the Edmonton Oilers on Saturday.

"We'll know more in the morning," coach Rod Brind'Amour said after that game. "Hopefully it's not overly serious." Ehlers has 22 points (six goals, 16 assists) in 23 games this season.

The Penguins are also short a star. Evgeni Malkin (upper body) did not travel and will miss his second straight game Sunday at Dallas. He is day to day and will be reevaluated Tuesday. He has 18 points in 19 games.

Vancouver's Elias Pettersson (upper body) is day to day and not expected to face the Chicago Blackhawks on Sunday. The center sat out Saturday's 4-3 shootout win over the Minnesota Wild and was still being evaluated. He has 23 points in 24 games, second on the team.

## More injury news around the league

- **Panthers:** Center Aleksander Barkov (knee) could return "somewhere in the next three games," coach Paul Maurice said before Saturday's 4-3 win over Buffalo. Forward Brad Marchand (knee) is also close, but Maurice expects Barkov back first.
- **Rangers:** Oliver Bjorkstrand and Alberts Smits are each out 2-3 months with upper-body injuries, the team announced Saturday. Defenseman Sean Durzi is a game-time decision against Carolina.
- **Kings:** Artemi Panarin could return Monday at Montreal. He was back on the ice for the morning skate after missing three games while away from the team following the death of his grandfather.
- **Ducks:** Center Leo Carlsson sustained an upper-body injury in the second period of Saturday's 3-2 loss at Utah after a collision along the boards. Coach Joel Quenneville said, "We'll see how he presents tomorrow."
- **Blackhawks:** Center Anton Frondell is questionable for Sunday's game at Vancouver after missing the third period and overtime of Saturday's win in Calgary.
- **Maple Leafs:** Forward Kirill Marchenko (lower body) was upgraded to day to day. He has missed 11 straight games, and Toronto has lost nine in a row (0-8-1).
