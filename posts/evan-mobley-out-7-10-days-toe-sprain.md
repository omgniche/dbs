Cleveland Cavaliers forward Evan Mobley will be sidelined for seven to 10 days with a sprained left great toe, the team announced in an official release Nov. 16.

Mobley got hurt Nov. 11 in New York against the Knicks. He then sat out the Cavs' next two games, at Miami on Nov. 13 and against Dallas on Nov. 14.

The Cavaliers' medical team did more evaluation, including MRI imaging, and that confirmed the sprain.

Mobley will now go through treatment and rehab for the next seven to 10 days. After that, he will be re-evaluated. The team said his status will be updated as appropriate, so watch for the next word once that window closes.
