The Atlanta Falcons traded safety Jessie Bates to the Jacksonville Jaguars for two draft picks, according to NFL.com's league news roundup.

Atlanta is 3-6, while Jacksonville is 6-2.

## Also around the league

In Cleveland, quarterback Deshaun Watson is expected to start Sunday against Houston, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported. Watson was inactive in Week 9 even after clearing concussion protocol, and Shedeur Sanders started in the loss to New Orleans.

In New England, coach Mike Vrabel said the team plans for receiver A.J. Brown to travel to Germany for next Sunday's game, though no decision has been made yet. Brown has missed the last two games.
