Oklahoma City's Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Miami's Giannis Antetokounmpo were both ruled out of Sunday night's Thunder-Heat game in Miami, the Associated Press reported. It was the first game either star has missed this season.

For fans of either team, it's a big hit. The game was missing two MVPs and two of the league's top four scorers this season.

Gilgeous-Alexander, the two-time reigning MVP, sat with a neck strain and a lower back contusion. Those come from a hard fall he took Friday night against New Orleans. "It wouldn't be wise for us to put him on the court tonight," Thunder coach Mark Daigneault said.

Antetokounmpo, a two-time MVP in 2018-19 and 2019-20, was out with a right groin strain. He went through pregame warmups but was ruled out about an hour before game time.

## Thunder already short-handed

Oklahoma City has been hit hard by injuries. Jalen Williams is out with an ankle injury that is possibly season ending, and Chet Holmgren is out with a knee injury until likely January. Alex Caruso, Cason Wallace, Jaylin Williams, Nikola Topic and Thomas Sorber were also out Sunday.

Gilgeous-Alexander came into the night as the NBA's No. 2 scorer at 31.7 points per game, behind only Luka Doncic of the Lakers at 37.4. He and Isaiah Hartenstein are the only two players to start all of Oklahoma City's first 16 games. Antetokounmpo is fourth in the league at 28.2 points per game.

Miami's next game is an NBA Cup matchup Wednesday at Philadelphia.
