The Hawks have two injury updates. Guard CJ McCollum has a left calf strain, and forward Asa Newell had surgery on his left big toe, the team announced.

McCollum was hurt during the Hawks' game at Milwaukee. After a medical evaluation and imaging, the team diagnosed the calf strain. He will be re-evaluated in about four weeks, and Atlanta says it will give an update then.

McCollum is in his second season with the club and is averaging 14.5 points in 26.4 minutes per game.

Newell hyperextended his left big toe Nov. 4 at Washington. On Nov. 16 he was re-evaluated by Dr. Martin O'Malley at the Hospital for Special Surgery in New York.

The new imaging confirmed the hyperextension and also showed a plantar plate tear in the toe. Newell had surgery with Dr. O'Malley on Nov. 17, according to the team. He will be re-evaluated in roughly four months, and the Hawks say another update will come at that point.
