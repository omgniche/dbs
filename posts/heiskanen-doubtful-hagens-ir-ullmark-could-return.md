Stars defenseman Miro Heiskanen is doubtful for Saturday's game against the San Jose Sharks, NHL.com reported in its Nov. 7 status report. The Bruins also placed rookie forward James Hagens on injured reserve Saturday, and Senators goalie Linus Ullmark could return the same night against Utah.

Those are big names for three fan bases. Heiskanen has 11 points in 15 games and averages 24:53 a night, and Hagens is tied for the NHL rookie lead with 12 points. Ullmark is 7-1-0 with a 1.33 goals-against average and a .945 save percentage.

## Stars and Bruins

Heiskanen missed Thursday's 2-1 shootout loss at Columbus. He left early in the second period of Tuesday's 6-5 win at Washington after Justin Sourdif hit him into the boards. Dallas doesn't play again after Saturday until Nov. 12. "If he's ready tomorrow, we would use him, but I think that's doubtful. We'll see," coach Glen Gulutzan said Friday.

Hagens was hurt in Boston's 6-2 win at Pittsburgh on Thursday. He left early in the third period after a cross-check to the back from Samuel Girard. His injury is undisclosed. Forward Mikey Eyssimont was recalled from Providence ahead of Sunday's game against Florida.

## Senators, Panthers and Islanders

Ullmark has missed seven games with a lower-body strain suffered Oct. 21 against Florida. He practiced fully Friday. "Haven't talked to our medical team yet, but he looked good today, so we'll see where he's at," coach Travis Green said. Forward Ridly Greig will miss a second straight game.

Florida forward Anton Lundell is day to day and won't play at Washington on Saturday. Cole Reinhardt will take left wing on the third line with Eetu Luostarinen and Lars Eller.

For the Islanders, Mathew Barzal and Matias Maccelli practiced in regular jerseys Saturday but aren't ready to return. Barzal has yet to make his season debut after reaggravating a knee injury before training camp. Coach Peter DeBoer said both would see the medical team before any decision on travel.

## Flames

Calgary's Jonathan Huberdeau skated in a regular jersey Friday but remains on injured reserve as he recovers from hip surgery. Coach Ryan Huska said there is "No timeline."
