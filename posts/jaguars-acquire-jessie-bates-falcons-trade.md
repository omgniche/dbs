The Jacksonville Jaguars are acquiring safety Jessie Bates from the Atlanta Falcons, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Monday. Jacksonville is sending Atlanta a 2027 pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

For Jaguars fans, this is a veteran safety joining the secondary in the middle of the season. Bates, 29, has started all nine games for the Falcons this year. He has 60 tackles, an interception and a fumble return for a touchdown.

The Falcons are paying most of Bates' $6.5 million salary to get the deal done, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported.

Bates appeared to confirm the move on social media Monday. He posted "ATL Forever Grateful!" to Atlanta and followed it with "WOKE UP A JAGUAR!"
