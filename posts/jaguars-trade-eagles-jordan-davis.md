The Jacksonville Jaguars are trading for Eagles defensive tackle Jordan Davis, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Monday. Jacksonville is sending Philadelphia a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick.

For Jaguars fans, this is the third defensive trade of the day. Jacksonville also acquired Falcons safety Jessie Bates and Saints defensive tackle Davon Godchaux. The run of moves has the Jaguars going all-in on a Super Bowl push.

Around The NFL will have more on the trade soon.
