Jake Bauers is leaving Milwaukee for Boston. MLB.com's Mark Feinsand confirmed a report that the first baseman has agreed to a four-year, $58 million contract with the Red Sox.

For Brewers fans, it's a loss on the field and a gain in the Draft. Milwaukee gave Bauers a qualifying offer, and he is signing elsewhere for more than $50 million. That means the Brewers stand to receive a compensation pick between the end of the first round and the start of Competitive Balance Round A next year, MLB.com reported.

## A career year, then a big October

Bauers hit .266/.389/.484 with a team-high 26 home runs in his age-30 season. He kept going into the postseason and won NLCS MVP. MLB.com pointed to his big at-bats against the Padres in the NLDS and the Dodgers in the NLCS during the Brewers' run to the first World Series title in franchise history.

Milwaukee has been in this spot before. After the 2024 season, shortstop Willy Adames signed a seven-year, $182 million deal with the Giants after getting a qualifying offer. The Brewers got the No. 32 pick in the 2025 Draft and took California prep shortstop Brady Ebel. He is now their No. 4 prospect and No. 93 on MLB Pipeline's Top 100.

## Who plays first now?

Bauers leaves a hole at first base. Andrew Vaughn, the right-handed-hitting complement to the left-handed Bauers, is in his final year of arbitration eligibility this winter.

In the Minors, MLB.com named 23-year-old Blake Burke, the Brewers' No. 12 prospect, as the leading candidate. The left-handed hitter put up a .263/.350/.538 line with 34 homers and 29 stolen bases between Double-A Biloxi and Triple-A Nashville in 2026.
