The Jets lost 31-12 to the Buffalo Bills at MetLife Stadium on Sunday, dropping to 2-8, the team said in its game recap. Buffalo improved to 8-1. Rookie quarterback Cade Klubnik made his professional debut in the second half after Geno Smith was hurt.

For Jets fans, the day mixed another loss with a quarterback situation that is suddenly unsettled. Smith was evaluated for a concussion after a hit at the end of the first half. He cleared protocol and came back briefly to lead a field-goal drive, then went to the locker room late in the third quarter with an ankle injury. Smith finished 13-of-21 for 148 yards.

Klubnik finished 11-of-20 for 137 yards and one interception. His first drive covered 61 yards in 10 plays and ended with a 22-yard field goal from Jason Sanders. He hit Garrett Wilson for 29 yards on the drive and scrambled for two first downs. Later, he was sacked twice on one possession, and he fumbled a pitch to tight end Kenyon Sadiq near the Bills' 23-yard line. Buffalo recovered, and James Cook ran 74 yards for a touchdown on the next play to make it 31-12.

## Wilson, Hall and the defense

Wilson led the Jets with seven catches for 106 yards, and Breece Hall topped 100 scrimmage yards. Sanders kicked field goals of 41, 27 and 22 yards, and the Jets had four field-goal drives in all.

Rookie edge David Bailey had two pass breakups, the first rookie edge with two in a game since 2017. Azareye'h Thomas intercepted Josh Allen in the fourth quarter, and Will McDonald IV sacked Allen. The Jets punted after Thomas's pick, though.

Buffalo led 10-6 at halftime. Allen scored on a 1-yard run in the first quarter, helped by a defensive pass interference call on Malachi Moore that cost the Jets 17 yards. Tyler Bass added a 37-yard field goal. In the second half, Allen hit Dalton Kincaid for 54 yards before scoring on another run, then threw a 45-yard touchdown to DJ Moore. Left guard Dylan Parham was injured in the first half and replaced by Jordan Meredith.

The Jets play road games at the Chargers and Dolphins before their Week 13 bye.
