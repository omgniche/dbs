The Jets have ruled wide receiver Garrett Wilson out for Sunday's Week 9 game against the Kansas City Chiefs because of a concussion, the team said in its Friday injury report.

Wilson did not practice Wednesday, Thursday or Friday.

There is better news in the backfield. The team's report headline says running back Breece Hall is set to return from a quad injury. Hall was limited Wednesday and then took full reps Thursday and Friday.

## Other Jets notes

Linebacker Trevin Wallace is also out with a groin injury. He was limited Wednesday and did not practice Thursday or Friday.

Jets listed as questionable include edge rusher Joseph Ossai (ankle), safety VJ Payne (knee) and defensive lineman Harrison Phillips (ankle). Payne was designated for return from reserve/injured and took full reps all week. Phillips returned to full practice Friday, and Ossai was limited all three days.

On the Kansas City side, the Chiefs ruled out cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder), defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) and defensive lineman Peter Woods (neck). Quarterback Patrick Mahomes (knee) and tight end Travis Kelce (ankle) both practiced fully Thursday and Friday and have no game status listed.
