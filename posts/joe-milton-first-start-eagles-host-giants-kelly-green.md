Joe Milton is making his first NFL start on Nov. 8 as the Philadelphia Eagles host the New York Giants at Lincoln Financial Field, the team said in its live coverage of the game. Kickoff is at 1:00 PM EST.

The Eagles are 2-6 and come in with a change at quarterback, hoping to spark a comeback in the tightly contested NFC East before their bye week. The Giants (4-2-1) are first in the division.

Philadelphia is wearing its Kelly Green throwback jerseys. New York is in its vintage white color rush uniforms and is looking to continue its momentum, according to the Eagles.

This won't be Milton's first time facing the Giants. He relieved Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott in Week 18 last season, and this game will be his second career appearance against New York.
