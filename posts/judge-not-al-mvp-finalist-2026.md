The 2026 AL and NL MVP announcements are underway on MLB Network and MLB+, MLB.com reported, and Aaron Judge isn't a finalist. Judge or Shohei Ohtani has taken MVP honors in each of the past five seasons, so MLB.com says at least one new name will likely get a trophy.

That matters because this could end a long run of the same two stars winning. MLB.com's preview doesn't include the winners, but it lays out who is in the mix.

## AL finalists

The AL finalists are Astros DH Yordan Alvarez, Rays third baseman Junior Caminero and Royals shortstop Bobby Witt Jr. Witt will finish in the top four of the voting for the third straight year.

Alvarez won the AL batting title at .316 and led the league in on-base percentage (.432), slugging (.601), OPS (1.033), total bases (341), walks (109) and extra-base hits (77). He hit 42 homers and drove in 106 runs, both career highs.

Caminero, 23, was the best player on the AL's best team, according to MLB.com. The Rays won 98 games, and he started all 162 at third base.

Witt scored 94 runs, fewer than Alvarez (105) and Caminero (100). MLB.com points out that the Royals ranked 22nd in the majors with 690 runs scored.

## NL finalists

The NL finalists are Cubs outfielder Pete Crow-Armstrong, Dodgers two-way star Ohtani and Brewers second baseman Brice Turang. MLB.com says Crow-Armstrong seems like the favorite after a big year at the plate and in the field.

Crow-Armstrong led the majors with 10.6 fWAR, 3.6 ahead of Witt in second at 7.0. MLB.com notes that not since 1956, when Mickey Mantle topped Duke Snider, had there been a gap of at least 3.0 WAR between the No. 1 and No. 2 players in baseball.

Ohtani is going for his fifth career MVP and his third in three seasons with the Dodgers. He hit 30 homers with an .889 OPS and a 143 OPS+. On the mound, he went 8-2 with a 1.79 ERA over 85 2/3 innings.

Turang set career highs with 21 homers, 100 RBIs, a .366 on-base percentage and a .450 slugging percentage. He also stole 19 bases and played Gold Glove defense at second base, MLB.com said.
