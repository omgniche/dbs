Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert is in the NFL's concussion protocol, head coach Jim Harbaugh said Monday, according to the team. Herbert reported symptoms to trainers and doctors after Sunday's game against the Jets.

"After the game, reported symptoms to the trainers and the doctors," Harbaugh said. He added: "Justin will be in the concussion protocol."

This matters because Herbert has to work through every step of the protocol before he can be cleared, and the Chargers (1-9) host the Patriots at SoFi Stadium in Week 12. The team hasn't said whether he will be ready.

If Herbert can't go, DJ Uiagalelei is the only other quarterback on the active roster. He made his NFL debut in the fourth quarter of Week 9 and threw for 24 yards and a touchdown. Veteran Jake Browning is on the practice squad.

"[Uiagalelei] would be the next man up, and then we would bring Jake Browning up as well," Harbaugh said.

## Other injuries

Running back Keaton Mitchell hurt his knee in the first quarter against the Jets and was questionable to return. He came back in the middle of the second quarter, returned a kickoff and finished with four snaps on offense and special teams. "We'll see how he responds to treatment this week," Harbaugh said.

First-round pick Akheem Mesidor left in the second quarter with an ankle injury after 12 defensive snaps. "Akheem, ankle, [we'll] see how he responds this week to treatment," Harbaugh said.

Daiyan Henley, who didn't practice last week because of a knee injury, was ruled out, and Tarheeb Still, added to the injury report Friday, also sat out. "They'll keep attacking the rehab and we'll see where the week takes us with Daiyan and Tarheeb Still," Harbaugh said.
