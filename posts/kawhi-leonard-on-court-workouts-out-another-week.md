Kawhi Leonard has started on-court workouts, but the Raptors said Tuesday he will be out at least one more week with swelling in his left knee.

The team said the forward "continues to make progress" after treatment. It plans to give another update in a week.

For Toronto fans, this is more waiting on a star who just came home. Leonard has been out since Oct. 23 and has played only two games this season. In those games he averaged 19.5 points, 7.5 rebounds and 3.5 steals, while shooting 32%.

Stepping onto the court is a clear move forward. Last week, the team said Leonard had "progressed in his strength and conditioning program following treatment to address left knee swelling."

## Back in Toronto

The 35-year-old is a two-time NBA Finals MVP. He led the Raptors to their only title in 2019. The Los Angeles Clippers traded him back to Toronto this summer. Soon after, he agreed to a $115 million extension that, at his option, could run through the 2028-29 season.

The Raptors' next update on Leonard is due in about a week, per Tuesday's announcement. The Associated Press contributed to the original report.
