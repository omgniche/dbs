Kirk Cousins is back under center for the Minnesota Vikings. Head coach Kevin O'Connell announced Friday that Cousins will start Sunday at the Green Bay Packers, with kickoff set for 1 p.m. ET.

It comes just five days after Minnesota got him in a trade with the Las Vegas Raiders. Cousins will wear No. 8 again, the number he had in his first run with the team from 2018 to 2023.

For Vikings fans, this is the team's bid to fix an offense that has lagged behind a top-tier defense. Minnesota is first in points allowed and second in yards allowed, but ranks 27th in points scored and 31st in yards.

O'Connell had not committed to Cousins on Wednesday. That was before Carson Wentz struggled in Monday night's loss to the Buffalo Bills, finishing 8 of 25 for 81 yards with three turnovers and an 8.9 passer rating. Wentz had stepped in after Kyler Murray hurt his hamstring.

O'Connell praised how Cousins has handled his first week back. "Cannot give him enough credit for coming in here, how he's gone about the week, both him and the coaching staff getting him ready to go, using all the time we possibly can," he said.

Cousins and O'Connell worked together in 2022 and 2023. Cousins made the Pro Bowl three times as a Viking, in 2019, 2021 and 2022. This season with Las Vegas, he completed 202 of 315 passes (64.1%) for 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions.

## A familiar stadium

The game is at Lambeau Field, where Cousins' last start for Minnesota ended badly. He tore his Achilles there in a Vikings win on Oct. 29, 2023.

The Vikings are 6-2 and tied for second in the NFC North, a division with four winning teams. The Packers are 6-3.
