Lakers guard Austin Reaves left Wednesday's 123-112 win over the Bucks early in the third quarter with a left heel injury and will get further imaging Thursday, NBA.com reported.

Head coach JJ Redick said X-rays came back negative, per a post from Dave McMenamin. There is no further update on Reaves until he has medical imaging back in Los Angeles. NBA.com's report also refers to the problem as an ankle injury, so the exact diagnosis is still unclear.

Reaves came into the night averaging 25.2 points.

Reaves went up for a dunk attempt early in the third quarter, appeared to hurt the heel and did not return. Before that he had 18 points, five assists, three rebounds and three steals in 25 minutes. He shot 6-for-10 from the field and 1-for-3 from three-point range.

He played 51 games in 2025-26 and dealt with multiple calf strains, plus a left oblique strain late in the season.

Luka Dončić also tweaked his ankle late in the game but avoided significant injury, Redick said.

NBA.com said the story will be updated as more information comes in.
