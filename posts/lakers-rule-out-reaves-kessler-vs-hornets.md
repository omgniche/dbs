The Lakers will play the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday night without guard Austin Reaves and center Walker Kessler. Los Angeles ruled both out Monday night, the Associated Press reported.

The Lakers have won eight straight games. Reaves is averaging 24.7 points, 14th in the NBA, in his first pro season without LeBron James as a teammate. Kessler is the team's prized offseason pickup.

Reaves bruised his heel last week in a win at Milwaukee. He then missed Sunday's 131-123 win at Phoenix. Tuesday will be his third missed game this season.

Kessler will sit out for the first time this year with right lower leg soreness. In his first 14 games with the Lakers, the 7-foot-2 big man is averaging 14.5 points, 10.9 rebounds and 2.2 blocks while shooting 68% from the field.

NBA scoring leader Luka Dončić is not listed on the injury report. He has missed two games this season.

The Hornets game starts a stretch of four home games in seven days for Los Angeles. Last season, Dončić played only 64 games because of various injuries, and Reaves was limited to 51.
