Lamar Jackson is expected to miss at least six weeks and as many as nine after surgery on a fractured and dislocated right thumb, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported Sunday. At the long end of that range, he would return around the start of the playoffs.

That gives Ravens fans some hope after a rough stretch. Baltimore is 4-5, last in the AFC North, and Rapoport reported that Jackson told teammates after Week 8 that he plans to return later this season. He was first reported to be out indefinitely.

The Ravens have already moved to cover the gap. With Jackson and Tyler Huntley (out for the year with turf toe) on injured reserve, undrafted third-stringer Joe Fagnano started Thursday night against Jacksonville, and it did not go well. Baltimore then acquired Jacoby Brissett from the Cardinals on Friday.

## Why Brissett, and what's ahead

Brissett is 33 and started all eight of Arizona's games. He was benched midway through the Cardinals' Week 8 comeback win over Dallas, dropped to third string the next day and then traded. He has completed 63.3% of his passes for 1,719 yards, 10 touchdowns and seven interceptions.

The schedule gives him a window. Over the next five weeks, the Ravens face the Chargers, Panthers, Texans and Buccaneers, who have a combined 8-21 record. Baltimore then closes with four games in the AFC North.

## A season of injuries for Jackson

This is Jackson's first year under coach Jesse Minter. He has started five games and finished three. He left the Week 4 game with an ankle sprain and missed three weeks. In Week 8, in his first game back, he was knocked out on the first snap when his right hand hit a Bills defender.

Jackson has completed 68 of 97 passes for 967 yards, six touchdowns and one interception. He also has 144 rushing yards and a touchdown.
