LeBron James will not play Monday night when the Philadelphia 76ers visit Brooklyn, the Associated Press reported. He was ruled out for low back/sciatica management on the second night of a back-to-back.

The absence fits a pattern this season. James, in his 24th year in the league and his first with Philadelphia, has not yet played both games of a back-to-back set.

The news comes right after a milestone night. On Sunday, James moved into third place on the NBA's career assists list, passing Jason Kidd. He finished with 15 points and 11 assists in a 131-124 win over Indiana and now has 12,092 career assists. He turns 42 next month.

There is good news for Philadelphia in the lineup. Center Joel Embiid is back in the starting five after sitting out Sunday. Dean Wade will start in James' spot.
