LeBron James will sit out the Philadelphia 76ers' game against the Toronto Raptors on Thursday night because of low back/sciatica management, The Associated Press reported.

The Sixers get some rest time to work with. James will have four to five days off before his next game, since Philadelphia is off Friday and Saturday. The team then plays back-to-back games Sunday and Monday.

James, who turns 42 next month, typically skips at least one game of a back-to-back, according to the AP. That means he could be back Sunday against Indiana or Monday at Brooklyn. Nothing in the report confirms which game, if either, he will play.

He has averaged 15.1 points, 7.2 rebounds and 6.5 assists in 10 games for the Sixers this season.

Philadelphia's injury report has another name on it. Center Joel Embiid is listed as questionable with right foot plantar fascia injury management.

Toronto is dealing with its own absences. The Raptors will again be without Kawhi Leonard (knee) and Scottie Barnes (groin).
