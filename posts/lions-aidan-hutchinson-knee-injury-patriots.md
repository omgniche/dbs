Detroit Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson was ruled out of Sunday's game against the New England Patriots in Munich with a knee injury.

Hutchinson got hurt in the first quarter. He sacked Patriots quarterback Drake Maye deep in Detroit territory, a play that forced a New England field goal. He then jumped in celebration, went down when he landed and grabbed his right knee. He walked off with trainers to the blue medical tent and then went to the locker room.

Maye also hurt his ankle on the play and was ruled out in the first half.

## Why it matters for Lions fans

If Hutchinson misses time, the Lions will feel it. The 6-2 Lions are in a bitter fight atop the NFC North with the 7-2 Bears, the 6-2 Vikings and the 6-3 Packers.

Hutchinson had one sack on nine snaps before he left. Coming into the game, the two-time Pro Bowler had five sacks and 18 QB hits in eight starts.

He has already come back from one major injury. Hutchinson missed 12 games in 2024 with a broken tibia and fibula. He bounced back in 2025 with a career year: 14.5 sacks, 35 QB hits and an interception in 17 games.

The way he was hurt, after the whistle during a celebration, recalls former Cardinals kicker Bill Gramatica, who tore his ACL after performing his trademark field goal celebration in a Week 14 game against the Giants in 2001.
