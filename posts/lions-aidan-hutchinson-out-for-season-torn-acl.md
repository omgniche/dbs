Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson tore his ACL in the first quarter of Sunday's win over New England in Munich, head coach Dan Campbell confirmed Tuesday. He will need surgery and is done for the season.

"It's what it appears to be, unfortunately," Campbell said. He added that Hutchinson will "come back better than ever next year."

The loss hurts a defense that has been rolling during a five-game win streak. Detroit has held its last five opponents to 14.4 points per game, and none of them reached 100 rushing yards or scored a rushing touchdown.

Campbell said more may be learned once surgery is done. "There's a little bit more in there," he said, adding that he doesn't know how significant it is.

The Lions do have some pass-rush help. Defensive lineman Alim McNeill leads the team with 6.5 sacks. Edge rusher DJ Wonnum has 6.0 and rookie edge rusher Derrick Moore has 4.0. Hutchinson also had 6.0, and the Lions are third in the NFL with 32.0 sacks. Without his total, they would still be tied for eighth.

Campbell said the team is better prepared for this than it was in 2024, when Hutchinson broke his leg in Week 6 in Dallas. "It's the reason why we have the depth we have in that room," he said. "We feel really good about that room."

He called on several players to do more, including McNeill, Wonnum, Moore, Skyler Gill-Howard and Tyleik Williams. "We will pick up the slack. It's going to require a lot out of everybody," Campbell said. He also noted that the secondary and linebackers have played better during the win streak.
