Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson has a torn ACL. Head coach Dan Campbell confirmed the news Tuesday on 97.1 The Ticket in Detroit, NFL.com reported.

Campbell said testing showed Hutchinson suffered the injury during Sunday's win over the Patriots in Germany. The Lions are 7-2.

## Joseph close to returning

There was better news on the injury front. Safety Kerby Joseph, who has been dealing with a knee issue, is expected to make his season debut in Week 11 against the Buccaneers, according to Campbell.

"He's done a really good job," Campbell said. "The knee has responded well. The plan is to get him going to let him play."

Joseph has not played since Week 6 of the 2025 season, NFL.com noted.
