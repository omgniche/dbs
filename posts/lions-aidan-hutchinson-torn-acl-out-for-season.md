Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson will miss the rest of the season with a torn ACL in his right knee, NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport reported Monday. Rapoport added that Hutchinson likely has other damage in the knee, possibly to the meniscus.

That's a huge blow for a 7-2 Lions team chasing the first Super Bowl trip in franchise history. They now have to try it without their top pass rusher.

Hutchinson got hurt in the first half of Sunday's 35-10 win over the Patriots at Allianz Arena in Germany. He had just sacked Drake Maye on third down to force a field goal on New England's first drive, then jumped to celebrate, as he often does. His right knee buckled on the landing. He walked off with the training staff and did not return.

He had an initial MRI in Munich and will have more tests in the United States.

## What Detroit loses

Hutchinson has six sacks this season, tied for second on the team, after a career-high 14.5 last year. His 19 QB hits lead Detroit by a wide margin, and his 50 pressures rank second in the NFL, per Next Gen Stats. The Lions are also without linebacker Jack Campbell.

There was some good news Sunday. Detroit still got four sacks after Hutchinson left: two from Derrick Moore and one each from Alim McNeill and D.J. Wonnum. McNeill had six pressures against New England and is having a career year as a pass rusher, with 35 pressures, 6.5 sacks and 13 quick pressures, all career highs through Week 10.

This is the second time in three years an injury has cut short Hutchinson's season. He missed 12 games in 2024 with a broken tibia and fibula.

The Lions play the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Week 11.
