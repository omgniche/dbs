Evgeni Malkin will miss the Pittsburgh Penguins' game against the Nashville Predators on Saturday with an upper-body injury, according to NHL.com's status report from Nov. 20. He is day to day, and the team said it will update his status Tuesday.

Malkin is second on the Penguins with 18 points (six goals, 12 assists) in 19 games.

Pittsburgh also put forward Nicholas Robertson on injured reserve with a lower-body injury. He will be reevaluated in at least two weeks. Forward Avery Hayes was called up from Wilkes-Barre/Scranton and is expected to play Saturday.

There was good news on Bryan Rust. He practiced in a noncontact jersey Friday for the first time since he got hurt Oct. 19. He was expected to be out six weeks and is still on that timeline.

"It's awesome to have him out there with us," coach Dan Muse said.

## Around the league

**Utah Mammoth:** Forward Dylan Guenther and defenseman John Marino both practiced Friday. Both have upper-body injuries and are day to day. Coach Andre Tourigny said "they're getting close" to playing. Guenther has missed five games and Marino 14. Utah wraps up a five-game homestand against the Anaheim Ducks on Saturday.

**Minnesota Wild:** Goalie Filip Gustavsson will make his season debut Saturday with Iowa of the AHL as he works back from offseason hip surgery, coach John Hynes said Thursday. In his absence, Jesper Wallstedt could start for the 22nd time in 25 games Saturday against the Vancouver Canucks, with Calvin Pickard as the backup.

**Tampa Bay Lightning:** Brandon Hagel (lower body) went on injured reserve Thursday after missing a second straight game, a 3-1 win over the Edmonton Oilers. Conor Geekie is also on injured reserve with an upper-body injury. Zemgus Girgensons is day to day with an upper-body injury. Jansen Harkins was recalled from Syracuse. Tampa Bay plays the Buffalo Sabres on Sunday.

**New York Rangers:** Forward Pavel Dorofeyev (lower body) skated on his own Thursday and remains day to day, coach Mike Sullivan said. He sat out Monday's 3-1 win over the Montreal Canadiens, his first missed game this season. Defenseman Sean Durzi, who has missed eight games, was a full participant in practice, a good sign he could return Sunday against the Carolina Hurricanes.
