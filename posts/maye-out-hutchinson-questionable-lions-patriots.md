Patriots quarterback Drake Maye has been ruled out of Sunday's Week 10 game against the Lions with an ankle injury. Detroit pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson is questionable to return with a knee injury.

For Patriots fans, it means New England is playing this one without Maye. For Lions fans, Hutchinson's status is unsettled while the game is still going.
