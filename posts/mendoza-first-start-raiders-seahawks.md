Fernando Mendoza will make his first start at quarterback for the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 10, hosting the Seattle Seahawks, Raiders.com reported Friday. The job is his after Kirk Cousins was traded to Minnesota.

It's a tough way to start. Seattle (5-4) is the defending Super Bowl champion and has the NFL's best defense, allowing just 244.2 yards per game, 155.1 of them through the air, and about 18 points per game. The Raiders come in at 4-5.

Head coach Klint Kubiak called it the best defense in the league and said Mendoza needs to do his job without worrying about everything else, while the players around him raise their level. Mendoza said he feels far more confident than when he arrived in Las Vegas, citing progress working under center, connecting with teammates and understanding the offense. He added there is still plenty of work left.

The rookie also said he, Cousins and Aidan O'Connell all prepared as starters, studying video and going over game plans together. Kubiak pointed to Cousins' preparation habits as a key lesson for Mendoza, from how he organizes his week to how he communicates with receivers.

## What the Raiders have going for them

Tight end Brock Bowers has 658 receiving yards and six touchdowns, the most productive tight end in the NFL this season. The offensive line has allowed only 11 sacks in nine games. The Raiders are also the only team that hasn't given up a sack when facing five or more pass rushers.

On defense, Las Vegas is looking to bounce back from giving up 38 points to San Francisco. The unit has 14 takeaways, fourth most in the league, and leads the NFL with 12 forced fumbles. Defensive coordinator Rob Leonard said the group knows what it wants to do and has shown it can, but needs more consistency.

The big test is Seahawks receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who has 1,036 yards and nine touchdowns in nine games. He's the only player in the league over 1,000 receiving yards.

The game is also a reunion for Kubiak, who faces his former team. He said he's grateful to see old friends and then it's time to play.

## Injury report

Seattle has ruled out quarterback Sam Darnold, offensive linemen Charles Cross and Quenton Nelson, and defensive lineman Leonard Williams. For the Raiders, Thomas Booker (ankle) and Dont'e Thornton Jr. (back) are questionable. Everyone else is available.
