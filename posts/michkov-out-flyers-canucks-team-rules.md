Matvei Michkov will not play for the Philadelphia Flyers against the Vancouver Canucks on Friday afternoon because of a violation of team rules, NHL.com reported. The team announced the move before the 3 p.m. ET game, calling it "an internal team matter" in a statement from Flyers governor Dan Hilferty.

Michkov leads Philadelphia with 11 goals and 20 points, so this is a big hole in the lineup for a team that is 9-12-6. The Flyers entered Friday fifth in the Metropolitan Division, five points behind the third-place New York Islanders.

Coach Rick Tocchet gave no details. "We're just going to worry about Vancouver," he said. Asked whether Michkov would play Saturday against the Islanders, Tocchet said, "We'll talk about this later."

## Rough stretch for the forward

Michkov has one goal in his last 11 games. He played a season-low 12:19 in Wednesday's 7-3 loss at Washington, and Tocchet said he noticed "some 10-second shifts for whatever reason." The coach said he did not see Michkov upset during or after that game.

This follows an argument between Michkov and defenseman Nick Seeler during a Nov. 9 game at Vancouver. The next day, NHL Network and ESPN analyst Kevin Weekes reported, citing league sources, that the 21-year-old likely prefers a fresh start elsewhere.

General manager Daniel Briere said Nov. 11 there was no truth to that report. He said Michkov remains a key part of the organization's future. "There's no doubt that we believe in him," Briere said.

Michkov, the No. 7 pick in the 2023 draft, led NHL rookies with 26 goals in 2024-25.
