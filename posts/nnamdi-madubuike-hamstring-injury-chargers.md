Ravens defensive lineman Nnamdi Madubuike left Monday night's game against the Los Angeles Chargers with a hamstring injury, the team's website reported. He was ruled out late in the third quarter.

Before going down, Madubuike had one tackle in the game.

This hurts because Madubuike only recently got back on the field. He returned in Week 3 after missing a full year with a neck injury, and the Ravens have leaned on him since. The All-Pro had played 73% of the defensive snaps going into Monday night.

Since his return, he has one sack in eight games, split between half sacks against the Browns and the Bills. He also has 11 tackles in that span.

The team's report didn't say how long he might be out or give any other details on the injury. We'll update this story when the Ravens say more.
