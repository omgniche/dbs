Green Bay Packers running back Josh Jacobs will have surgery on a fractured finger this week, coach Matt LaFleur told reporters Monday. LaFleur said Jacobs' return timeline is "to be determined."

Asked about placing Jacobs on injured reserve, LaFleur said, "I think everything's on the table." An IR stint would sideline him for at least four games.

The injury hits a Packers backfield that was already struggling. Jacobs is only three games into his return from the Commissioner Exempt List, where he spent the first six weeks of the season. The three-time Pro Bowler has 148 total yards on 47 touches, and his 2.2 yards per carry is the lowest average of his career. He left Sunday's blowout win over New England in the first quarter after gaining five yards on four carries.

Green Bay's run game is the league's worst, and it will lean on Kaleb Johnson, MarShawn Lloyd, Pierre Strong and Chris Brooks. Brooks has been on IR since Oct. 10. With Jacobs out and Lloyd inactive with a knee injury Sunday, Johnson, a second-year trade acquisition, ran for a career-high 68 yards on 14 carries.

## Off-field situation

Jacobs was placed on the exempt list after pleading no contest on Sept. 10 to charges of battery and criminal damage to property. He was arrested May 26 after his then-girlfriend said he had thrown her to the ground three days earlier. New allegations from years earlier surfaced in a records release last month. His lawyers say he has a "substantial body of tangible evidence" disputing them. The NFL's investigation is ongoing.

## What's next

Green Bay is 6-3 and in a tight NFC North race. It hosts the rival Vikings next Sunday before its Week 11 bye. After that, the Packers visit the Rams on Thanksgiving Eve, play at New Orleans in Week 13 and host the Bills in Week 14.
