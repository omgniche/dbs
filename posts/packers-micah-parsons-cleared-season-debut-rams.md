Micah Parsons is about to play again. The Packers pass rusher has been medically cleared and is set to make his season debut Wednesday night against the Rams, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported Monday.

Kickoff from SoFi Stadium is 8 p.m. ET. Parsons still has to be officially activated to the 53-man roster before then. He has been on the physically unable to perform list since returning to practice Nov. 4, and he was limited in Monday's session.

The timing matters for Green Bay. The Packers are 6-4 and last in the NFC North, but they hold the No. 7 spot in the playoff picture, tied in record with the reigning champion Seahawks and the Rams. Parsons told reporters Monday that he still needed final sign-off from Dr. Neal ElAttrache, who repaired his knee, and others, according to The Athletic. Rapoport's report suggests that clearance has now come.

Parsons will be back 346 days after he tore his ACL in Week 15 of the 2025 season. He later revealed that he also had a procedure to repair his meniscus. Green Bay was 9-3-1 and chasing a division title when he went down. The Packers then lost a wild-card game to Chicago and opened the 2026 season with a loss to Minnesota.

The defense could use the help. It has improved in its first year under coordinator Jonathan Gannon, but it allowed 30 points to the Kirk Cousins-led Vikings at Lambeau Field in Week 10.

Parsons' track record is strong. He was a first-team All-Pro in 2025 even though he missed the last three games. He also earned his fifth Pro Bowl nod in five seasons and finished third in Defensive Player of the Year voting. He has recorded at least 12 sacks in every one of his five NFL seasons.
