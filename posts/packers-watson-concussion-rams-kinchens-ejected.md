Packers receiver Christian Watson was ruled out of Wednesday night's Week 12 game against the Rams with a concussion. Rams safety Kamren Kinchens was ejected for the hit that caused it, with 1:54 left in the first half.

Green Bay lost its leading receiver in the game at the break, and Los Angeles lost a safety. Watson had a game-high four catches for 65 yards, including the Packers' only touchdown, when he left.

The play came on fourth-and-1. Jordan Love threw toward the sideline, Watson tracked the ball, and Kinchens knocked him down. Watson stayed on the ground for a while, then walked on his own to be evaluated for a concussion. He was first listed as questionable to return and then ruled out at the start of the second half.

According to the broadcast, the ejection call came from the league office in New York and NFL executive vice president Troy Vincent. The reason was that Kinchens left his feet and made helmet-to-helmet contact.

There was no penalty on the field, because the hit was offset by an offensive pass interference call on Green Bay's Matthew Golden. Trey Smack then kicked a game-tying field goal for the Packers, and the teams went to halftime tied 13-13.

Kinchens has played in nine games with four starts. He had a tackle and two pass breakups when he was tossed. Watson's 6-yard touchdown catch from Love came with 8:06 left in the second quarter.

This is not Watson's first scary moment this season. In Week 8 against the Panthers, he came out of an ambulance after being taken to a hospital as a precaution, and he returned to play.
