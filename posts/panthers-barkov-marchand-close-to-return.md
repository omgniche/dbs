Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand are nearing a return for the Florida Panthers, coach Paul Maurice said after Sunday's practice, according to the team. Both still need final clearance.

"We're getting pretty close," Maurice said. He added that he was impressed by both players in practice, saying they are getting through the work and their numbers are good.

For Panthers fans, this means the captain and one of the team's biggest veteran names could be back in the lineup soon. Both have been out since the first week of the season.

Barkov, 31, suffered a lower-body injury in the second game of the year at San Jose on Oct. 1. He played 20:32 in Florida's 1-0 win at Carolina to open the season. He missed all of last season with a knee injury, and he is the franchise leader in goals (286), assists (496) and points (782).

Marchand went down on Oct. 6 at Los Angeles, two games into the year. Kings defenseman Brandt Clarke high-sticked him as he drove to the net, and he slid into the right post with a lower-body injury. He had offseason surgery in August after an injury ended his 2025-26 season last March.

Defenseman Aaron Ekblad said the team is eager to have them back. "We can't wait," Ekblad said. He called Barkov "a calming presence in the center of the ice" and said "Brad is an energy guy."

## What's next

The Panthers have Monday off, then plan a tough practice Tuesday at Baptist Health IcePlex. Asked whether either player could face the Ottawa Senators on Wednesday, Maurice said, "It's possible." He said Tuesday's session should give the team "a good idea of where we're at."
