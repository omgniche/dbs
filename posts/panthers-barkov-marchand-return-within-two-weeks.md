Florida Panthers forwards Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand are back on the ice, and coach Paul Maurice said Tuesday they could return to the lineup "within two weeks." Both skated with the team for the first time since getting hurt, NHL.com reported.

Barkov hurt his left knee Oct. 1, in the second game of the season, after missing all of last year with a knee injury. Marchand was injured Oct. 6 when he fell into the goal post during a 2-1 win at the Los Angeles Kings.

Maurice said doctors gave a window of six to eight weeks for the pair. Barkov hits the six-week mark Thursday, with Marchand just under it. "I think we're within two weeks of getting those guys back," Maurice said.

Defenseman Seth Jones said seeing them skate helps the room. "It gives everybody a lift to see those guys on the ice," he said.

## Around the league

The Minnesota Wild get captain Jared Spurgeon back Tuesday against the Montreal Canadiens after he missed five games with a lower-body injury. Forward Michael McCarron also returns.

The St. Louis Blues activated defenseman Logan Mailloux from injured reserve, and he will make his season debut Tuesday against the Detroit Red Wings. He had surgery on his right hand Sept. 27. "I'm just happy to be done with it," Mailloux said Monday.

Detroit also activated forwards Marco Kasper and Mason Appleton from injured reserve, and both are expected to play against St. Louis. Coach Todd McLellan said the team has to "temper our expectations" while still expecting them to be energized.

The Toronto Maple Leafs have several players on the mend. Defenseman Morgan Rielly is day to day with a lower-body injury. Forwards Kirill Marchenko and Colton Sissons and defensemen Nick Blankenburg and Marshall Rifai are all week to week.

The New York Rangers listed rookie defenseman Alberts Smits, the No. 5 pick in the 2026 draft, as week to week with an upper-body injury. Colorado's Cale Makar will play Tuesday at Toronto after leaving Sunday's practice as a precaution.

The Buffalo Sabres will also get Ryan McLeod back Wednesday against Tampa Bay after two games as a healthy scratch. Goalie Alex Lyon, who hasn't played this season, will travel with the team on its six-game road trip starting Thursday.
