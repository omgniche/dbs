Patriots quarterback Drake Maye is believed to have a significant lower-leg injury that could sideline him for the rest of the 2026 season, NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport reported, citing sources familiar with the situation.

Maye went down on New England's first drive of Sunday's game in Germany against the Lions, after a sack by Detroit edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson. Hutchinson also left the game with an injury, which came during the celebration.

Maye grabbed at his leg after the hit and walked off gingerly on his own before heading to the locker room. He was ruled out in the second quarter.

This is a major blow for a team trying to follow up last season's run with another AFC title. Maye finished as the runner-up for 2025 NFL MVP, and his possible absence is the latest in a string of injuries that has derailed New England's season.

After the 35-10 loss, coach Mike Vrabel said he had no update on the severity of the injury. He said the club would "get more tests run" once it returned to the United States.

Backup Tommy DeVito took over and struggled. He led only one scoring drive, in the fourth quarter with the game already out of reach, and the offense had seven three-and-outs. DeVito finished 12 of 24 for 81 yards with a touchdown, an interception and four sacks. Detroit's pass defense entered the day ranked 32nd in the league in passing yards allowed per game.

The Patriots fell to 5-5 and head into their bye week behind the Browns and Steelers in the playoff race. Without Maye, climbing back into position would be a tall order.
