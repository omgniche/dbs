Patriots quarterback Drake Maye is out for Sunday's game against the Detroit Lions after hurting his ankle in the first quarter.

Maye appeared to get hurt when Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson sacked him. Hutchinson was also injured on the play. Maye walked off on his own and went to the bench, then headed to the locker room with trainers.

Maye was initially deemed questionable to return, then downgraded in the second quarter and ruled out. Tommy DeVito is in at quarterback for the Patriots.

Maye, a third-year QB, had gone 3-of-4 for 48 yards and added an 11-yard scramble before he left.

The source did not say how serious the ankle injury is or how long Maye might be sidelined, and it gave no further details on Hutchinson's condition.
