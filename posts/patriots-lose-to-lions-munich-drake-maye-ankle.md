The New England Patriots lost 35-10 to the Detroit Lions in Munich on Sunday, and quarterback Drake Maye suffered an ankle injury that could keep him out of more games. It was New England's second blowout loss in a row and dropped the team to 5-5.

The reigning AFC champions are now at risk of missing the playoffs, and Maye has been propping things up elsewhere on offense. Medical testing on his ankle was set for Monday.

The defense has allowed 80 points over the last two games. Detroit piled up 494 yards after Green Bay gained 478 the week before. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for more yards than the entire Patriots offense gained.

A.J. Brown, the star offseason addition, struggled again and left the game with another injury. He has 11 catches on 26 targets this season. Cornerback Christian Gonzalez picked off a pass, but he was also at the center of two Amon-Ra St. Brown touchdowns, a 56-yarder after a missed tackle and a 28-yarder on a tunnel screen.

"Just not executing," Gonzalez said of the defense. He added: "We had a good week of practice. That doesn't really matter when we don't come out and execute on Sundays."

## What's next

New England heads into its bye week after returning to Foxborough. The break could give Maye time to rehab before a road game against the Los Angeles Chargers. After that, the Patriots face the Buffalo Bills, Minnesota Vikings and Kansas City Chiefs in succession.

Head coach Mike Vrabel said he wants his players to treat the post-bye stretch as a fresh 0-0 start. "This league isn't always 10-game winning streaks," Vrabel said.
