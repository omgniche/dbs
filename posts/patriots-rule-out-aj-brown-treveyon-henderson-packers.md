The Patriots ruled out wide receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) and running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness) on Friday for Sunday's Week 9 game against the Packers. Kickoff at Gillette Stadium is 4:25 p.m. ET.

Brown will miss his second straight game after a knee flare-up in practice last week. He has played in just three of New England's eight games this season, with nine catches for 101 yards. He also missed four games while on injured reserve.

The bigger worry for the offense is tight end Hunter Henry, who is questionable with a foot injury. He was injured in the second quarter of last week's game against Miami after just eight plays. Henry sat out the first two practices this week before returning in a limited role Friday.

Henry leads the Patriots with 29 catches and has 326 receiving yards, second on the team. Quarterback Drake Maye has never played a full game in his three-year career without him.

Six other players are questionable: S Kevin Byard III (chest), CB Carlton Davis III (neck), WR Mack Hollins (calf), OT Morgan Moses (foot), ST Brenden Schooler (hamstring) and OL Greg Van Roten (hamstring).

## Other items

Hollins and Van Roten are both on injured reserve with their 21-day practice windows open. The Patriots have until 4 p.m. ET Saturday to activate them. Hollins led the team in catches (15) and receiving yards (209) over the first four weeks.

On the other side, Packers star edge rusher Micah Parsons (knee) won't play Sunday as he works back from a torn ACL and meniscus.
