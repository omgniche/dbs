Jalen Duren will be out at least two more weeks with a hamstring injury, the Detroit Pistons announced Sunday. The All-Star center is expected to miss at least six more games.

Duren, 22, has already sat out six games since getting hurt in Detroit's second game of the season, a 128-111 win over the Miami Heat on Oct. 23. If the timeline holds, he will have missed at least a dozen games.

The Pistons did say Duren has been cleared for full basketball participation.

Duren signed a five-year, $200 million contract extension before this season. Last year was a breakout for him, as he averaged 19.5 points and 10.5 rebounds. His numbers dipped in the playoffs, where he put up 10.2 points and 8.5 rebounds over 14 games.

The Pistons made the announcement Sunday, according to the Associated Press.
