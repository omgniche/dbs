The Tampa Bay Lightning placed forward Brayden Point on injured reserve Tuesday, according to NHL.com's latest status report. Point left Sunday's 4-3 loss to the Winnipeg Jets in the first period with a lower-body injury.

Point has 16 points (five goals, 11 assists) in 17 games this season. Forward Sam O'Reilly was recalled from Syracuse of the AHL and is expected to make his NHL debut Wednesday against the Buffalo Sabres.

## Senators lose Cousins for a bit

Ottawa forward Nick Cousins will be out 7-10 days. He had to be helped off the ice with 2:57 left in Tuesday's 3-2 overtime loss to the Washington Capitals, after a hit along the boards from defenseman Jakob Chychrun.

"He's doing OK, I guess. As good as could be expected. He'll be out for a little bit though," Senators coach Travis Green said. Cousins has three points in 19 games.

## Fiala close for Kings

Los Angeles forward Kevin Fiala is nearing his season debut, which could come as soon as Thursday against the New York Islanders. He suffered multiple fractures in his left lower leg playing for Switzerland at the 2026 Winter Olympics in February, and had a follow-up procedure in September.

## Around the league

Vegas defenseman Noah Hanifin is out week to week with an undisclosed injury, coach Ryan Craig said Tuesday. He left Sunday's 4-1 win at Buffalo in the third period after a hit along the boards. Defenseman Parker Wotherspoon, who has missed 13 games, practiced in a regular jersey.

Calgary forward Brennan Othmann returns Wednesday against the New York Rangers after missing six games with an upper-body injury. Jonathan Huberdeau has been skating in a regular jersey, but coach Ryan Huska said there is still no timeline for his return.

Seattle defenseman Ryan Lindgren, out since Oct. 6, moved to a regular jersey at practice in Helsinki. He could return during the Global Series in Finland, though it is unclear whether he will play Thursday against the Carolina Hurricanes. Coach Lane Lambert said Wednesday he is "not 100 percent sure that he will, but definitely is trending (up)."
