Golden State Warriors center Kristaps Porziņģis announced Friday that he is stepping away from basketball to focus on his health, the Associated Press reported. The 31-year-old shared the news in a post on X.

Porziņģis did not say what the health matter is, and he did not make clear whether he plans to play again.

The announcement follows weeks of uncertainty. In late September, the Warriors said he would be out indefinitely with a health issue and would not travel with the team to Hawaii for training camp. Fans now have his own word on where things stand, though not many details.

Porziņģis wrote that he had hoped to play with this group, but that his body "didn’t respond the way I anticipated" as he ramped up. He said he is doing well but is not where he wants to be to play at the level he expects of himself.

He said he intends to work with the Warriors to "find a solution that makes sense." He ended his message by thanking his family, close ones and supporters, writing: "Onwards and upwards."

Porziņģis was the No. 4 pick by the New York Knicks in the 2015 Draft. Golden State is his sixth team in 11 seasons.
