The NFL suspended Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua for three games for violating its personal conduct policy, the league announced Thursday. The suspension starts right away, and he can be reinstated on Dec. 14 after the Rams' Week 14 game against the 49ers.

Nacua was already sidelined. He went on injured reserve Nov. 12 with knee and toe injuries he picked up late in the Week 9 win over the Commanders on Nov. 8. He had already been expected to miss at least the next three games.

## What led to the suspension

The discipline comes from a New Year's Eve 2025 incident in which Nacua is alleged to have made an antisemitic remark and bitten a woman. She filed a civil lawsuit against him for assault and battery on March 25.

Nacua's attorney denied that his client made any such statements. The attorney described the biting as "horseplay."

Nacua entered a holistic care facility earlier in March, before the lawsuit was filed. He finished his stay in time for the start of the Rams' offseason program on April 20.

## Where things stand

Nacua, a BYU product from Las Vegas, was a fifth-round pick by the Rams in 2023. He is a two-time Pro Bowler, including his rookie year.

This season he has played in seven games while dealing with multiple injuries, catching 70 passes for 715 yards and four touchdowns. The Rams are 6-4 and on their bye.
