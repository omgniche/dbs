Quinn Hughes will not play for the Minnesota Wild against the Vancouver Canucks on Saturday night, NHL.com reported. The team has not disclosed what the injury is.

The defenseman left Thursday's 4-1 win at the Calgary Flames in the third period, after taking a hit from Flames forward Adam Klapka at 9:57. Wild coach John Hynes did not have an update on Hughes after the game.

## Why it matters

Hughes is on a roll. He has 21 points (six goals and 15 assists) during a 13-game point streak, and he had a goal and an assist in Thursday's win. Minnesota is 17-3-4 and has won five straight. The Wild are 11-0-2 over their last 13 games.

There is also a bigger storyline around him. Hughes, 27, is in the final season of a six-year, $47.1 million contract, per NHL.com. He has been eligible to sign a new deal since July 1 and could become an unrestricted free agent if he doesn't re-sign. Minnesota got him from Vancouver in a trade on Dec. 12, 2025.

## Guerin has made no decisions

Wild general manager Bill Guerin told NHL.com at the general managers meeting in Toronto on Nov. 10 that he would be willing to keep Hughes past the March 1 trade deadline without a new contract.

Guerin was asked by WCCO on Nov. 12 whether he had decided not to trade Hughes no matter what. He said, “No. I haven’t decided that. No. I haven’t decided anything.” Asked if he knows when he’ll decide, Guerin said, “No. I don’t.”
