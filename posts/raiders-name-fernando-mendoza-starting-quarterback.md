Fernando Mendoza is the Raiders' starting quarterback for Week 10 of the 2026 season. General Manager John Spytek named the rookie the starter after practice.

The move follows a trade that sent Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings for a 2027 third-round pick. For Raiders fans, it hands the keys to the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, who is now the man under center.

"This is something that he's worked very hard for since the moment he's walked into this building," Spytek said. "He passed every test."

Spytek also said Mendoza put the team in a spot where it had to make "a very hard decision today with his growth as a person, as a teammate, as a leader, as a quarterback."

Mendoza arrives with a big résumé. He won the Heisman Trophy and a national championship with Indiana in 2025. In his junior season, he completed 72 percent of his passes for 3,535 yards and led the nation with 41 touchdown passes.

In his one regular-season game so far, against the New England Patriots, Mendoza completed 75 percent of his passes for 48 yards.

Klint Kubiak said the quarterback's preparation made the trade possible. "He's prepared," Kubiak said. "He practices extremely well and his work habits also allowed us to make this trade because we feel that he's ready to go."

Kubiak added that the team is excited to see Mendoza "take the ball for the game here this Sunday."
