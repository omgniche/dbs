The Las Vegas Raiders have traded quarterback Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings for a third-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, the team announced Tuesday. The deal is not final until Cousins passes a physical.

For Raiders fans, the move sends a veteran starter out and brings back draft capital for next year. The team's announcement did not give a reason for the trade or say what it means for the quarterback position going forward.

Cousins started nine games for Las Vegas in 2026. He completed 202 of 315 passes (64.1%) for 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions, good for a 97.1 passer rating. He also ran for two touchdowns.

The 15-year veteran has played in 183 games, with 176 starts, since entering the league in 2012. Over that stretch he has completed 4,136 of 6,214 passes (66.6%) for 47,040 yards, 318 touchdowns and 139 interceptions, with a career passer rating of 96.8. He has also scored 22 rushing touchdowns.

The Raiders' announcement did not say anything further about the terms of the trade beyond the 2027 third-round pick.
