Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua will likely land on injured reserve after suffering an MCL and PCL sprain and turf toe against the Commanders, head coach Sean McVay said Monday.

"He's going to be out for a good amount of time," McVay said. Pressed on a timeline, he said "it's a strong chance that we put him on IR. So we're thinking it's going to be at least a month."

Nacua had a season-high 150 receiving yards and a touchdown on 14 catches Sunday. He now has 70 catches for 715 yards and four touchdowns in seven games this year.

He appeared to get hurt on the Rams' last offensive play at Washington, finishing a jet sweep as the ball carrier. McVay took responsibility for the call.

"I'd be lying to you if I said I haven't questioned the decision making on that last play over and over in my head of what could have been another option that you felt like could have gotten a conversion and maybe ended the game that would have led to him not taking a violent hit to his right knee," McVay said.

## Avila, Ferguson and the IR math

Offensive lineman Steve Avila left the game with a hamstring strain. McVay said he'll need some time and added, "hopefully we'll get him back at some point." Putting Avila on IR is "not something we're trying to do right now," McVay said, because the Rams are getting close to the limit of eight IR return slots per team in the regular season.

The Rams have used three of those slots so far. Tight end Terrance Ferguson, who pulled a hamstring in Friday's practice, is a separate case. He is in his 21-day practice window, and McVay said the team hopes to avoid not activating him when it ends. McVay also said Ferguson would probably miss a couple more weeks.

McVay has previously said a return slot will go to defensive end Kobie Turner. Wide receiver Davante Adams also factors into the count.

## McDuffie progressing

There was better news on cornerback Trent McDuffie, who missed the Commanders game with a back injury suffered the week before against the Chargers. McVay said he is "making good progress with his back" and the team hopes to have him back this week.
