The New York Rangers will be without forward Oliver Bjorkstrand and defenseman Alberts Smits for 2-3 months each because of an upper-body injury, the team announced Saturday, according to NHL.com's latest status report.

That's a big hit for a Rangers group that has been off to a strong start. Bjorkstrand has 11 points in 22 games and got hurt midway through the third period of a 3-1 win over Montreal on Nov. 16. Smits, the No. 5 pick in the 2026 NHL Draft, has no points in 17 games and averages 14:29 a night.

There was other Rangers news. Pavel Dorofeyev (lower body) went on injured reserve Saturday, but he skated on his own that morning and remains day to day. He has 12 points in 21 games. Defenseman Sean Durzi, who has missed eight games, could return Sunday against Carolina. Max Sasson, acquired from Vancouver, practiced with the team, and Luke Glendening was expected to be placed on waivers.

## Barkov close, Malkin out

Florida captain Aleksander Barkov (knee) could be back "somewhere in the next three games," coach Paul Maurice said before Saturday's game against Buffalo. Barkov got hurt in the Panthers' second game this season, after he missed all of last season with a knee injury. Brad Marchand (knee) is also close, but Maurice expects Barkov to come back first.

The Panthers host Ottawa on Wednesday, visit Washington on Friday and host Tampa Bay on Nov. 28.

In Pittsburgh, Evgeni Malkin sat out Saturday's game against Nashville with a lower-body injury. He is day to day, and the Penguins said they will update his status Tuesday. He has 18 points in 19 games, second on the team. Nicholas Robertson (lower body) went on injured reserve and will be reevaluated in two weeks, and Avery Hayes was recalled from the AHL.

Bryan Rust (lower body) practiced Friday in a noncontact jersey for the first time since getting hurt Oct. 19. He is still on his projected six-week timeline. "It's awesome to have him out there with us," coach Dan Muse said.

## Around the league

- **Maple Leafs:** Kirill Marchenko (lower body) was upgraded to day to day and joined the morning skate in a noncontact jersey. He will miss his 11th straight game.
- **Wild:** Goalie Filip Gustavsson was set to make his season debut with Iowa of the AHL on Saturday, as he comes back from offseason hip surgery.
- **Blues:** Connor McMichael (undisclosed) missed the game against New Jersey but could return Sunday against Edmonton, coach Jim Montgomery said.
- **Islanders:** Defenseman Ryan Pulock (upper body) was out at Detroit and will be evaluated once the team gets home.
