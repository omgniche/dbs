Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey has been deactivated for Sunday's game against the Carolina Panthers, head coach Jesse Minter announced Thursday.

Humphrey was sent home after making a derogatory comment about Baltimore on his podcast. He won't practice this week, and Minter said the deactivation covers one game.

For fans, this takes a full-time starter off the field at a rough moment. Humphrey has started all 10 Ravens games this season, and the team is trying to end a five-game losing streak.

Minter said Chidobe Awuzie and T.J. Tampa will take Humphrey's place against Carolina.

The coach also spoke about how Humphrey has responded. "He's very remorseful. I think he really understands that words mean a lot, especially to people in this city," Minter said.

Minter added that the comment doesn't match what the team wants to stand for. "When you say something like that, it's not a reflection of our team and our organization and how we want to operate," he said.
