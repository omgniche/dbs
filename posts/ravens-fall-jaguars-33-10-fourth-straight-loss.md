The Baltimore Ravens lost 33-10 to the Jacksonville Jaguars on Thursday night, extending their losing streak to four games and dropping to 4-5, the team's website reported Friday.

Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano made his first NFL start on a short week, playing with a heavily wrapped right ankle. Jacksonville scored on its opening possession and again just before halftime, and Baltimore never recovered. The Ravens have been outscored 104-33 during the skid.

The night also brought more bad injury news. All-Pro safety Kyle Hamilton left in the third quarter with a hip injury, and head coach Jesse Minter had no update afterward. Cornerback Nate Wiggins was scratched before the game with an illness/stinger.

## What the pundits saw

The Athletic's Jeff Zrebiec noted that Fagnano threw two interceptions and averaged 3.3 yards per pass attempt. Tight end Mark Andrews lost a fumble, and LaJohntay Wester fumbled a kickoff. Pre-snap penalties and big plays allowed by the defense were again problems. The Baltimore Sun's C.J. Doon wrote simply, "This is rock bottom."

There were a few bright spots. Jacksonville went just 4-for-12 on third down and managed 77 rushing yards, well below its average. The Ravens ran for 172 yards against the league's top run defense, including a 65-yard touchdown by Justice Hill in the fourth quarter, according to Russell Street Report's Rob Shields.

## Brissett coming, Jackson timeline unclear

The Ravens placed Lamar Jackson on injured reserve before the game, so he will miss at least four games. Minter declined to say whether Jackson has had surgery or if his return timeline is any clearer.

ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported that Jacoby Brissett was headed from Arizona to Baltimore to finalize a reported trade. "I'm told that he knows, or has the expectation, that Lamar Jackson could be back at some point late in the season, so he's looking at this as really a five-to-six game run to try to keep the seat warm for Lamar if he can make it back," Fowler said.

The Ravens' own report did not specify when Brissett would take over at quarterback.
