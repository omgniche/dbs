The Baltimore Ravens finalized a trade Friday with the Arizona Cardinals for veteran quarterback Jacoby Brissett. Baltimore also gets a 2027 fifth-round pick and sends Arizona a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick.

The move comes at a hard time for the Ravens. Lamar Jackson is out for at least four games and Tyler Huntley is done for the season. Brissett's job is to keep Baltimore afloat until Jackson is back.

He brings experience the roster lacked. Brissett has 73 starts across 11 NFL seasons with the Cardinals, Patriots, Commanders, Browns, Dolphins and Colts. Before Baltimore's Week 9 game against Jacksonville, the three healthy quarterbacks on the roster, Joe Fagnano, Austin Reed and Kedon Slovis, had zero combined starts and 23 combined regular-season pass attempts.

## What Brissett brings

He can handle volume. Since the start of last season, Brissett ranks fifth in pass attempts. In Week 11 of 2025, he set an NFL regular-season record with 47 completions in a game. This season he has six games with at least 35 attempts, and he completed 65.6% of his passes in those games.

The Ravens' passing game should open up. In the Jaguars game, Fagnano threw 18 passes and only one traveled at least 10 air yards, per Next Gen Stats. Brissett's air yards per attempt since 2025 is 7.7, just under the eight average for quarterbacks with at least 500 attempts in that stretch.

There are risks. Brissett has thrown seven interceptions this season, five of them under pressure, compared to one pressured pick all of last year. He has also taken the fifth-most sacks since the start of 2025. Baltimore's offensive line, which has struggled at times in pass protection, will need to be better.

His career interception rate is 1.5%. Among quarterbacks with at least 2,500 attempts this century, only Aaron Rodgers is lower, according to Pro Football Focus.

## Familiar faces and a short-yardage fix

Brissett is listed at 6-foot-4 and 235 pounds and was the quarterback in an early version of the "Tush Push" for the Colts in 2020. The Ravens have struggled in short-yardage situations this season.

He will also see some familiar faces. Quarterbacks Coach Israel Woolfork was with him in Arizona last season and in Cleveland in 2022. Running Backs Coach Eddie Faulkner was an assistant at NC State when Brissett played there in 2014 and 2015.
