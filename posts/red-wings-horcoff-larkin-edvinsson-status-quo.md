Detroit Red Wings interim GM Shawn Horcoff said Tuesday that the situations with Dylan Larkin and Simon Edvinsson are "status quo," NHL.com reported from the league's general managers meeting in Toronto. It was Horcoff's first GM meeting, and he is trying to earn the job permanently.

The update matters because the Red Wings have two core players in limbo while the team sits last in the Atlantic Division. Detroit is 7-8-3 and has missed the playoffs 10 straight seasons, the longest active drought in the NHL.

## Larkin still out, Edvinsson unsigned

Larkin, 30, asked for a trade after last season and has used the no-trade clause in his contract to limit Detroit's options. He left the team Oct. 29 and is suspended without pay. He is in the fourth year of an eight-year, $69.9 million contract.

Horcoff would not say whether Larkin has changed the list of teams he would accept a trade to. "Ideally, we get something that works for both sides, right?" Horcoff said. "But it has to be something that helps this team."

Edvinsson, 23, is an unsigned restricted free agent working out in Nashville. He reportedly wants to be traded. He must sign by Dec. 1 to be eligible to play this season.

Horcoff has said he doesn't want to trade the defenseman, but he left the door open to calls. "I'd still like to sign Simon, but I think when it's someone like that and the situation that we're in, you can't help but take calls," he said.

## Scouting and standings

Horcoff played 1,008 NHL games from 2000 to 2016 and has been in Detroit's front office since 2016, the last five seasons as an assistant GM. He said he already knows the other GMs, either from playing with them or from years around them.

He said he and his staff have been scouting heavily on the road, and that it isn't only because of the Larkin and Edvinsson situations. Detroit is three points behind Florida for the second Eastern Conference wild card. "I think we're in a situation where we have to get better," Horcoff said. "I think it's no secret."

The Red Wings were scheduled to play at the St. Louis Blues on Tuesday night.
