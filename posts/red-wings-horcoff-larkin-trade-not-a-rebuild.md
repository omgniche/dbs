Red Wings interim general manager Shawn Horcoff said Monday that trading Dylan Larkin to the Montreal Canadiens is not the start of a rebuild, NHL.com reported. Detroit sent its former captain to Montreal on Sunday for forwards Michael Hage and Kirby Dach, goalie Sam Montembeault and four draft picks.

Those picks are a conditional first-rounder in 2027, a conditional first-rounder in 2029, a second-rounder in 2027 and a fourth-rounder in 2028.

For fans, the message is that Detroit still wants to end a playoff drought that goes back to 2015-16. Horcoff said the Red Wings plan to use the haul to improve the current roster, and that could mean trading for NHL-ready players.

"Again, I'm looking to make this team better in any way I can and for us to take the next step and become a playoff team," Horcoff said.

## What Detroit got

Horcoff said Hage, a 20-year-old in his junior season at the University of Michigan, was hard to pass up. Hage has 26 points in 13 games there. Dach, 25, was the No. 3 pick in the 2019 draft and has eight points in 22 games for Montreal this season. Horcoff said he has battled injuries but is playing well now.

Montembeault has not played this season after an undisclosed injury in early October. Horcoff said, "He's getting healthy. He's close to returning." Detroit already has John Gibson and Daniil Tarasov, but Horcoff said extra goalie depth is "never a bad thing to have."

Horcoff also pointed to Grand Rapids prospects Carter Bear, Nate Danielson and Axel Sandin-Pellikka as players close to the NHL. The Red Wings also want to build around Moritz Seider and Lucas Raymond.

## How it got here

Larkin asked for a trade in the offseason. The team announced Sept. 11 that he would attend camp but would no longer be captain. He played 10 games, left the team Oct. 29 and was suspended without pay. Detroit drafted him No. 15 in 2014. He makes his Canadiens debut Monday.

The Red Wings are 8-12-3 and have lost seven of their last eight. They host Vancouver on Wednesday at 7:30 p.m. ET. Horcoff said he has liked the team's effort but that the club is struggling to score.
