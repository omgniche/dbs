The Detroit Red Wings traded forward Dylan Larkin to the Montreal Canadiens on Sunday, NHL.com reported, ending a saga that led to his suspension in late October.

Detroit gets forwards Kirby Dach and prospect Michael Hage, goalie Sam Montembeault, and four picks: conditional first-rounders in 2027 and 2029, a 2027 second-rounder and a 2028 fourth-rounder. Montreal also gets a 2028 second-round pick and a 2027 third-round pick.

Larkin asked for a trade over the summer, and the request became public. Detroit said Sept. 11 that he would no longer be captain, a job he had held since Jan. 13, 2021.

Larkin missed the preseason and two regular-season games with an upper-body injury. He came back Oct. 6 and had six points (two goals, four assists) in 10 games. On Oct. 29, interim general manager Shawn Horcoff said Larkin had told the team he would no longer play for Detroit, and the team suspended him.

The 30-year-old Larkin, a Waterford, Michigan, native, leaves with 649 points (278 goals, 371 assists) in 818 regular-season games. Detroit drafted him 15th overall in 2014. The Red Wings made the playoffs once in that span, losing to Tampa Bay in five games in 2016. He has five seasons left on an eight-year, $69.6 million deal signed March 1, 2023.

Dach, 25, has eight points in 22 games for Montreal this season. He signed a one-year, $3.6 million contract on July 16, 2026. The Blackhawks took him third overall in 2019.

Montembeault, 30, has not played this season after an undisclosed injury in practice early in October. He is in the last year of a three-year, $9.45 million contract.

Hage, 20, is unsigned. The Canadiens drafted him 21st overall in 2024, and the University of Michigan center leads the NCAA with 26 points in 13 games this season.

Montreal (14-6-4) sits third in the Atlantic Division. Detroit (8-12-3) is seventh.
