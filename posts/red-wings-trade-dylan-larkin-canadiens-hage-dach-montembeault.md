The Detroit Red Wings traded forward Dylan Larkin to the Montréal Canadiens on November 23, the team announced. Detroit gets forwards Michael Hage and Kirby Dach, goaltender Samuel Montembeault and four draft picks. Along with Larkin, the Red Wings send Montréal a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick.

The Red Wings give up Larkin and take back three players plus a stack of future picks, including two conditional first-rounders.

## The picks

Detroit will receive Montréal's own 2027 first-round pick, plus a 2027 second-round pick. If Montréal's 2027 first lands in the top 10 after the draft lottery, the Canadiens keep it. Detroit would then get Montréal's 2028 first-round pick, which would be unprotected.

The same setup applies in 2029. Detroit gets Montréal's 2029 first-rounder unless it falls in the top 10 after the lottery. In that case Detroit gets the 2030 first instead, also unprotected. The Red Wings also get a 2028 fourth-round pick.

## The players

Hage, 20, is a junior at the University of Michigan. The team says he has 26 points in 13 games this season, the most among all NCAA Division I skaters, and his 21 assists also lead the group. Montréal picked him 21st overall in 2024. He won bronze with Canada at the 2026 World Juniors and made the tournament All-Star Team.

Dach, 25, has eight points in 22 games with the Canadiens this season. The Blackhawks took him third overall in 2019, and he has 144 points in 328 NHL games with Chicago and Montréal.

Montembeault, 30, went 10-8-4 with a 3.43 goals-against average and a .872 save percentage in 25 games last season. The year before, he went 31-24-7 with a 2.82 GAA and four shutouts in 62 games. He also won gold with Canada at the 2023 World Championship.
