The Detroit Red Wings traded Dylan Larkin to the Montreal Canadiens on Sunday, NHL.com reported, ending a standoff that began when he asked out after last season. Larkin agreed to the move.

Detroit gets prospect center Michael Hage, forward Kirby Dach, goalie Sam Montembeault, conditional first-round picks in 2027 and 2029, a 2027 second-rounder and a 2028 fourth-rounder. Montreal also gets a 2028 second-round pick and a 2027 third-round pick from Detroit in the deal. Dach and Montembeault are pending unrestricted free agents.

For Red Wings fans, Hage is the key piece. The 20-year-old, the No. 21 pick in 2024, leads NCAA Division I in scoring. He plays at Michigan, where Larkin also played, and has 26 points in 13 games as a junior.

The move also closes an ugly chapter. Larkin left the team Oct. 29 after 10 games, and Detroit suspended him without pay. He gave up about $1 million in salary. The Red Wings were 5-4-3 when he left and have gone 3-8-0 since. They sit at 8-12-3. They have missed the playoffs 10 straight seasons, the longest active drought in the league.

Montreal is getting a proven scorer. Larkin, 30, has scored at least 30 goals in each of the past five seasons. He is signed for five more years at $8.7 million per season. He and captain Nick Suzuki now give the Canadiens two top centers. Montreal reached the Eastern Conference Final last season and has 32 points, tied for second in the Atlantic.

Montreal fans chanted for Larkin when Detroit visited Oct. 10. "I never would have imagined that in this building in my career," Larkin said afterward.

He will face Detroit fans soon. His first trip back to Little Caesars Arena is Feb. 18.

The Red Wings still have business to settle with defenseman Simon Edvinsson, an unsigned restricted free agent who wants to be traded. He must sign by Dec. 1 to play in the NHL this season.
