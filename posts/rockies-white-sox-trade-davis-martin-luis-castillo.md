The Rockies have reportedly made an early offseason move, trading with the White Sox for right-handers Davis Martin and Luis Castillo. MLB.com's Mark Feinsand reported the deal, citing a source. Neither club has confirmed it.

Colorado would send left-hander Sean Sullivan and 17-year-old Venezuelan catcher Doug Veliz to Chicago. Sullivan is ranked No. 20 in the Rockies' prospect system, according to MLB.com.

For Rockies fans, the deal brings in two arms with very different recent track records. Martin was a key part of the White Sox rotation all season, making 28 starts with a 3.95 ERA and 122 strikeouts over 146 innings. He threw just one inning in the playoffs.

Castillo, a three-time All-Star, had a rough stretch after Chicago acquired him at the deadline. He made six starts for the White Sox with an 8.42 ERA and was left off their postseason roster. Chicago went on to reach the ALDS, where it lost to the Guardians in five games.

Colorado would take on Castillo's full 2027 salary, which is more than $24 million, per the report.

Watch for official word from the Rockies and White Sox on the trade.
