Sam Darnold is set to return at quarterback when the Seahawks visit the 49ers in Santa Clara on Sunday. He missed the last two games with quadricep and rib injuries.

Darnold returns after a full week of practice with no limitations, ahead of Seattle's Week 12 road game.

"I'm feeling good," Darnold said after Friday's practice. He called the 49ers "a really good football team" and said, "We're excited about the challenge."

Darnold also missed time early this season with a glute injury he suffered in the Week 1 win. He wouldn't compare how he feels now to that earlier return, saying only that he feels "really good right now."

He said the quad, more than the rib injury from early in the Week 8 loss to the Bears, kept him out. "I felt like I could have played the week after if it was just the rib," he said.

Coach Mike Macdonald said, "Sam did a great job. We got through the Wednesday, so we're ready to roll."

## More starters return, but two are out

Defensive end Leonard Williams is back after missing two games with a stinger. Left tackle Charles Cross returns after missing three games with an ankle injury.

Of Williams, Macdonald said: "It's great to have him back; he might be our best player, and he can wreck the game from inside and out, and he's done that."

Cross' return lets Josh Jones, who had been filling in at left tackle, slide to right tackle. He'll replace Abraham Lucas, who is out with an ankle injury. "Getting Charles back will be great, and much needed with Abe's situation too," Macdonald said.

Veteran defensive tackle Jarran Reed is also out. He hurt his toe in the Week 10 win in Las Vegas. Macdonald called it "a good opportunity for guys like Brandon Pili and Rylie Mills."
