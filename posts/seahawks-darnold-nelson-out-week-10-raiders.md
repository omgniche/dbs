Seattle quarterback Sam Darnold and left guard Quenton Nelson will not play in the Week 10 game against the Raiders, coach Mike Macdonald said Monday, a day after the Seahawks beat the Cardinals 17-3.

Nelson had a minor procedure on his knee Monday. Darnold is dealing with quadricep and rib injuries.

The Seahawks won Sunday without Darnold, Nelson, left tackle Charles Cross, defensive tackle Leonard Williams, outside linebacker Uchenna Nwosu and receiver Rashid Shaheed. For the rest of that group, Macdonald said nothing is settled yet.

"I'd say we haven't ruled anybody out yet, but I think everybody needs to make progress," Macdonald said. "So nobody's in, nobody's out."

## Darnold could return after the bye

There is some good news at quarterback. Darnold has a shot to be back after Seattle's Week 11 bye. Asked about that, Macdonald said, "I think so," and added, "He's making progress, it's just we're going to need some more time."

Drew Lock hurt his oblique Sunday after entering the game with an elbow injury. Macdonald said the team expects him to play against the Raiders, though his practice work will be limited again. Jalen Milroe is also part of the mix in practice.

"He's not taking all the reps, and he's not taking all the throws," Macdonald said of Lock's workload. He added that Seattle will manage it day to day based on how Lock feels.

## Nelson week to week, Williams unclear

Nelson, who Seattle acquired in a trade with the Colts before the Week 8 game, is considered week to week. The Seahawks are not planning to put him on injured reserve, which would suggest they don't expect him to miss four or more games.

"We're not putting him on IR yet, so I think that gives you the timetable of what we expect," Macdonald said.

Williams is dealing with a stinger, and Macdonald said it's hard to pin down a return date. "They vary in severity," he said, adding that it's "definitely not a straightforward pathway on how we move forward."
