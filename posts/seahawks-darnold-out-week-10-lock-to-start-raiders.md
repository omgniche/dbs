Sam Darnold will not play for the Seahawks in Week 10 against the Raiders, and Drew Lock is expected to start at quarterback for the second week in a row. Coach Mike Macdonald shared the news Monday on Seattle Sports radio. Darnold is recovering from quad and rib injuries.

This is the third game Darnold will miss this season. He started every game over the past two years, first with the Vikings and then the Seahawks. For fans, it means the offense stays with the backup who just led the team to a win.

Lock is dealing with elbow and oblique injuries of his own, but Macdonald said he had a "good exam" Monday. On the oblique, the coach said, "I think it's something that we can manage."

In Sunday's 17-3 win over the Cardinals, Lock went 19 of 28 for 175 yards and a touchdown. It was his second start of the season in place of Darnold. He played despite tendinitis in his right elbow. Across three appearances this year, he has completed 71.1% of his passes for 597 yards and five touchdowns, with no interceptions.

Rookie Jalen Milroe is set to back up Lock again.

Seattle is 5-4. It is still unclear whether several starters who missed the Week 9 game will be back, including Rashid Shaheed, Leonard Williams and Uchenna Nwosu. The Seahawks play in Las Vegas on Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET, then head into their Week 11 bye.
