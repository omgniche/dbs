Drew Lock will start at quarterback for the Seahawks again Sunday against the Raiders, with Sam Darnold still out, coach Mike Macdonald said Wednesday, per Seahawks.com. Darnold is recovering from quadricep and rib injuries that also sidelined him for last weekend's win over the Cardinals.

For fans, the big question is how healthy Lock is. He has an elbow issue that has limited him in practice for a few weeks, plus an oblique injury that popped up during Sunday's game. Macdonald said he is progressing and will follow a plan like last week's.

"Drew's making good progress, so we have a plan, similar to last week and we'll go from there," Macdonald said.

Last week, Lock shared practice reps with second-year quarterback Jalen Milroe, and the team capped his throws so the elbow would feel good on game day. Macdonald said that approach continues.

"He's not going to take every rep, he's not going to make every throw again, but that's just trying to be as prudent as possible through the process to make sure he's feeling his best on Sunday," Macdonald said.

## Nelson out, others may return

Newly acquired guard Quenton Nelson will also miss the game. He had a procedure on his knee Monday and is back with the team. Macdonald sounded upbeat.

"He's here, he's back in the building," Macdonald said. "He looks good, we were able to get to some things, treatment wise, that are of the chasing edges caliber, which is music to my ears." He added that the team will take things "week by week at this point."

There was better news on several others who sat out last week: left tackle Charles Cross, receiver Rashid Shaheed, outside linebacker Uchenna Nwosu, defensive tackle Leonard Williams and nose tackle Brandon Pili. Macdonald said they would get some work Wednesday, and the team would see how they look Thursday.

"We haven't ruled anybody out yet, nor have we ruled anybody in yet, but good news on a lot of those guys," Macdonald said.
