Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said Monday that quarterback Sam Darnold is likely to return for Seattle's Week 12 game against the 49ers, following the bye week. Darnold has missed the past two games with rib and quadricep injuries, according to Seahawks.com.

For fans, the message is that Darnold is the starter again as soon as he's healthy. Drew Lock has played well in relief, including in Sunday's win over the Raiders in Las Vegas, but Macdonald left no doubt about the pecking order.

"Drew has been fantastic, he has been phenomenal, but Sam's our quarterback," Macdonald said on his weekly show on Seattle Sports 710AM.

Macdonald was careful about the timeline at his press conference. "The best way to put it right now is we're optimistic that he's going to get a full week's worth of reps next week and play the game," he said. He added that Darnold couldn't do that right now and that the team is assuming his recovery keeps progressing.

## Why a healthy Darnold matters

Darnold helped lead Seattle to a Super Bowl title earlier this calendar year. He is also coming off back-to-back Pro Bowl seasons in which his teams won 14 regular-season games.

This season hasn't gone smoothly for him. Macdonald pointed to Darnold getting hurt in the first series of the opener, and later to the Chicago game. He said Darnold took a rib injury on the first drive there, then played the full game anyway. Macdonald said the Kansas City game showed the best version of Darnold he has seen, and that it was probably the best the offense has been all season.

Macdonald said being "fully healthy is going to help," and that the bye week gives the team a chance to sharpen how it wants to operate down the stretch. He said he can't wait for Darnold to get a full week of work and be healthy.

Seattle's Week 12 game against the 49ers is the first chance to see it.
