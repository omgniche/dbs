The Pittsburgh Steelers announced late Tuesday night that Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos. Mason Rudolph will start at quarterback instead.

This matters in the AFC North race. Pittsburgh is 6-4 and has lost two straight, scoring fewer than 20 points in both games. The Steelers also visit M&T Bank Stadium in the final week of the regular season, and Rodgers' future status with the Steelers remains to be seen.

ESPN's Brooke Pryor reported that Rodgers missed a Wednesday morning meeting. Pryor also reported that head coach Mike McCarthy made the call, hoping Rudolph can spark the offense. NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported that Rodgers was not at the team facility Wednesday morning and that some players are unhappy with the sudden move.

Gerry Dulac of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette reported that the situation had been "brewing for a while" and "reached the point of no return" Tuesday night.

Rodgers has said this will be his last season after a 22-year career. He turns 43 next week. Sunday's 19-16 loss to the Eagles may have been his final game.

This season he has completed 60.9% of his passes and been sacked 31 times, already more than last season's 29. His 257 yards lost on sacks lead the NFL.

## Bengals fall to Commanders

Cincinnati's five-game winning streak ended Monday night, when Washington beat the Bengals 22-20 on a walk-off field goal. Jayden Daniels hit Antonio Williams twice for 64 yards, setting up Drew Stevens' 26-yard kick as time expired.

Joe Burrow threw for 300 yards and two touchdowns. Ja'Marr Chase caught five passes for 31 yards. "One loss doesn't determine nothing," Chase said, via the Bengals' website.

The Bengals (7-3) still lead the division by one game over both Pittsburgh and Cleveland (6-4).

## Watson open to Browns future

Deshaun Watson has led six game-winning drives for the Browns, who hold the No. 7 seed in the AFC. He is in the last year of his five-year, $230 million deal. On his contract, Watson said, "That's up to my agent."

Cleveland hosts the Raiders in Week 12 in a game flexed to Sunday night.
