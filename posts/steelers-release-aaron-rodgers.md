The Pittsburgh Steelers have released quarterback Aaron Rodgers, the team announced on Nov. 25.

Rodgers started 10 games for Pittsburgh this season. He completed 220 of 361 passes for 2,241 yards and 16 touchdowns.

The release ends a two-year run for Rodgers in Pittsburgh. The team's announcement gave no reason for the decision and said nothing about who will take snaps going forward.

## A look back at his time in Pittsburgh

Rodgers first joined the Steelers on a one-year contract in 2025. He started 16 games that season, completing 327 of 498 passes for 3,322 yards and 24 touchdowns. The team re-signed him in the offseason.

## Career numbers

The team's announcement also noted Rodgers' résumé over a 22-year career. He has started 267 of the 274 games he has played. In that time he has completed 9,104 passes for 68,515 yards and 543 touchdowns, with 129 interceptions.
