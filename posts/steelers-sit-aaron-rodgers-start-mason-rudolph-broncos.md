The Pittsburgh Steelers announced late Tuesday night that quarterback Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos. Mason Rudolph will start in his place.

Pittsburgh has lost two games in a row. NFL Network's Cameron Wolfe reported that Rodgers has been benched.

Rodgers was not on the team's Tuesday injury report.

For now, Rudolph is the starter Friday against Denver, and Rodgers will be on the inactive list.
