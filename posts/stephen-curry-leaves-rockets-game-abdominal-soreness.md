Stephen Curry was ruled out for the rest of the Warriors' game against the Rockets on Wednesday with right abdominal soreness, NBA.com reported, citing multiple reports. He went to the locker room after shooting a 3-pointer and was later ruled out with what was described as abdominal tightness.

The timing is rough for Golden State. The Warriors are 8-3 and riding an 8-game winning streak, and Curry came into the night averaging 30.6 points per game, second in the NBA. He got hurt 5:35 into his performance against Houston.

His health has been a storyline before. Curry, 38, played 70-plus games in back-to-back seasons, but he got into only 43 games last season because of a lingering knee issue.

NBA.com said the story will be updated as more information becomes available. For now, the report doesn't say how serious the soreness is or what it means for Curry's next game.
