The Tennessee Titans placed running back Tyjae Spears on injured reserve Saturday, the team announced. Spears had been on the injury report with a knee injury.

The move changes the running back room heading into Sunday's game against the Cowboys. The Titans also said running back Michael Carter will be a gameday elevation for the contest.

Spears played in seven games for Tennessee this season. He had 22 carries for 94 yards.

There was good news on the defensive side. Safety Tony Adams cleared concussion protocol and carries no injury designation into the Cowboys game, per the team.
