DJ Uiagalelei will start at quarterback for the Chargers on Sunday against New England, head coach Jim Harbaugh announced Friday. Justin Herbert has been ruled out of the Week 12 game with a concussion.

It will be the first NFL start for Uiagalelei, who is in his second year with the team. Herbert did not practice this week after entering the league's concussion protocol following last week's game against the Jets.

Uiagalelei, 25, made his regular season debut in Week 9 against the Texans. He came in during the fourth quarter and led a touchdown drive late in the game, throwing the first touchdown pass of his career to Derius Davis.

## A winding road to the starting job

Uiagalelei went undrafted after the 2025 NFL Draft and spent his rookie season on the Chargers' practice squad. He started this season there too. The team then signed him to the active roster after Trey Lance was placed on Injured Reserve.

In college, he played 53 games over five seasons at Clemson, Oregon State and Florida State. He threw for 9,384 yards, 61 touchdowns and 30 interceptions with a 128.3 passer rating, and added 21 rushing touchdowns.
