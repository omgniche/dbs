Kirk Cousins is heading back to Minnesota. The Vikings are getting the 38-year-old quarterback from the Las Vegas Raiders for a 2027 third-round pick, NFL Network Insiders Ian Rapoport and Mike Garafolo and ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Tuesday, and the team has since announced the deal.

For Vikings fans, this is a big swing at fixing an offense that has been a mess. Minnesota has the league's top scoring defense, but the offense ranks 27th in points, 31st in yards and 31st in passing. The Vikings have put up 16 points or fewer in five of their eight games, including the last three.

Neither Kyler Murray nor Carson Wentz changed that. J.J. McCarthy, drafted in 2024 to replace Cousins, was traded to the Giants on Sept. 28.

Cousins played for Minnesota from 2018 to 2023 and reunites with head coach Kevin O'Connell, who led the team for Cousins' last two seasons there. In 2022, their first year together, Cousins threw for 4,547 yards, 29 touchdowns and 14 interceptions and went 13-4.

## What it means for the Raiders

Cousins has played well in Las Vegas. He has 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions, and the Raiders are 4-5 and on the playoff bubble.

Fernando Mendoza, the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 draft, now takes over. The former Indiana Heisman winner and national champion has appeared in one game this season. He came in during a blowout loss to the Patriots and went 6-of-8 for 48 yards.

If Cousins is ready for Week 10, he would face the Packers at Lambeau Field. That is the same place he played his last game as a Viking in 2023.
