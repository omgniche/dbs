The Minnesota Vikings are placing receiver Jordan Addison on injured reserve, head coach Kevin O'Connell announced Monday. Addison is expected to return in about four weeks.

Addison already sat out Sunday's 30-10 win at Green Bay. O'Connell said he hurt his thumb in Friday's practice while catching balls on air, and called it "a freak thumb injury."

## What Vikings fans should know

The injury aggravated something Addison dealt with in training camp, according to O'Connell. Addison will have minor surgery to fix it.

The coach said Addison tried to play against the Packers. "He wanted to try to go," O'Connell said, but "just couldn't get there."

O'Connell doesn't see this as a major setback. "I don't anticipate it being a long-term kind of thing," he said. He compared it to the recovery of Donovan Jackson, who missed three games last season after wrist surgery.

O'Connell did note one difference. Addison is a receiver who has to catch the football, which makes his situation "a little bit different there."

The team made the announcement Monday.
