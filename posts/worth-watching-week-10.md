Every week we rank the games that are actually worth your Sunday. The score is simple: how much is at stake, how much these teams dislike each other, who's on the field and how likely it is to get weird.

Notice what's missing. A blowout you had money on isn't a good game, and a 13–10 rock fight between rivals can be the best three hours of the week.

## 1. Eagles at Cowboys

Holiday stage, real hate and two offenses that can go off at any moment.

## 2. Dolphins at Patriots

A division game in the cold with plenty riding on it.

## 3. Packers at Bears

The oldest rivalry in the league never needs an excuse.

## 4. Chiefs at Broncos

Altitude, a loud building and a matchup that always seems to end close.

## 5. 49ers at Seahawks

Two teams that know each other too well, in a stadium built to rattle visitors.
